# 追加：第 17 節第 10 條，老人遛狗怎麼牽繩

2026-10-09，issue #100「對養寵物的建議」拆出來的一處。主題是老人牽狗散步摔骨折。四問：老人遛狗在國內很常見；把繩子繞在手腕上、用伸縮繩放長是常見習慣，很少有人知道這會傷手、會被拽倒；動作是新的，全書原來只有第 2 節第 44 條（遛狗算進走路量）和第 8 節第 30 條（養狗必須牽繩），都不講怎麼牽；來源是美國疾控中心報告和同行評審論文。

全部數字只來自美國急診監測（NEISS）和美國一家手外科門診，國內沒有找到同類統計。

## 1. 美國 65 歲以上牽狗散步摔骨折
- 題錄：Pirruccio K, Yoon YM, Ahn J (2019). JAMA Surg 154(5):458-459. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamasurg.2019.0061>（PMID 30840050，PMC6537776）
- 這是研究快報，Europe PMC 沒有摘要，正文用無頭 Chrome 帶代理從 <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6537776/> 取得。
- 原文：「The annual number of patients 65 years or older presenting to US emergency departments with fractures associated with walking leashed dogs increased significantly between 2004 (n = 1671; 95% CI, 1111-2232) and 2017 (n = 4396; 95% CI, 3202-5590)」
- 原文：「Most fractures occurred in women (78.6%; 95% CI, 74.9%-82.2%). Most patients sustained hip fractures (17.3%; 95% CI, 13.8%-20.7%), although the upper extremity was the most frequently fractured region overall (52.1%; 95% CI, 48.1%-56.1%); 28.7% of patients (95% CI, 23.7%-33.8%) required hospital admission.」
- 原文（動作依據）：「advocating for preventive actions, such as obedience training to ensure dogs do not lunge while leashed, or suggesting smaller dog breeds for individuals contemplating ownership.」
- 沒收：討論裡「mortality rates approaching 30%」是作者引別人的髖部骨折死亡率，不是本研究的數，沒寫。

## 2. 全美急診狗繩相關受傷
- 題錄：Forrester MB (2020). Am J Emerg Med 38(9):1782-1786. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajem.2020.05.082>（PMID 32739848），摘要經 Europe PMC REST 取得。
- 原文：「A dog leash was involved in 8189 injuries, resulting in a national estimate of 356,746 injuries and an estimated rate of 63.4 injuries per 1,000,000 population. Of these injuries, 193,483 resulted from a pull, 136,767 from a trip/tangle, and 26,496 from other or unknown circumstances. The total injury rate per 1,000,000 population increased from 25.4 in 2001 to 105.5 in 2018.」
- 這份是全年齡的數，條目裡寫明是「和狗繩有關的受傷」，沒說成老人。

## 3. 手外科門診 443 例狗繩傷手
- 題錄：Plusch K, Givner D, Wiafe B, Lutsky K, Beredjiklian P (2026). Hand (N Y) 21(4):582-586. <https://doi.org/10.1177/15589447241299128>（PMID 39600039，PMC11600420），摘要經 Europe PMC，正文用無頭 Chrome 從 PMC 取得。
- 原文（摘要）：「We identified 443 patients that presented for a dog-leash-related injury.」「Surgery occurred following injury in 96 patients (21.6%).」「The majority of injuries were caused by the extremity catching in the leash while being pulled (337, 75.9%).」
- 注意：正文結果段寫的是「(334, 75.4%)」，和摘要不一致。條目按摘要寫，說人話只寫「約四分之三」。
- 原文（動作依據）：「the results of our study suggest that a leash should never be wrapped around the hand or fingers, as a sudden pull can lead to the common types of injuries seen in our practice. Maintaining the leash firmly in the palm of the hand is the safest way to avoid injury to the finger and wrist. However, this does not necessarily reduce the risk of falls following a sudden lunge by the dog. With larger dogs especially, a shorter leash length reduces the amount of time for the dog to build up speed, reducing the possibility of a strong pull to the owner once the dog reaches the end of the leash. Additionally, longer leashes increase the possibility of the leash wrapping around other objects and creating a tripping hazard for the owner.」
- 作者同時寫明「our study data do not provide information related to prevention of these injuries」，所以這是建議，不是研究結論。備註裡交代了「防不住被拽倒」這一句。

## 4. 美國疾控中心：和貓狗有關的跌倒
- 題錄：CDC (2009). MMWR 58(11):277-281（PMID 19325528）。<https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5811a1.htm>，curl 帶代理 403，無頭 Chrome 帶代理能取。注意 mm5811a2 是另一篇（鈉攝入），別引錯。
- 原文：「an estimated average of 86,629 fall injuries associated with cats and dogs occurred in the United States each year during 2001--2006」「Nearly 7.5 times as many injuries involved dogs (76,223 [88.0%])」
- 原文：「Twenty-six percent of falls involving dogs occurred while persons were walking them, and the most frequent circumstances were falling or tripping over a dog (31.3%) and being pushed or pulled by a dog (21.2%).」
- 原文（動作依據）：「reinforcing American Veterinary Medical Association recommendations emphasizing obedience training for dogs ( 3 ) to minimize behaviors associated with falls (e.g., pushing or pulling).」

## 5. 伸縮繩
- 任務原稿要求寫「別用伸縮繩」。查下來沒有一份來源直接比較伸縮繩和普通繩的摔倒風險。Rosa R, Buckley RE (2024). JAVMA 262(7):973-978. <https://doi.org/10.2460/javma.23.11.0608> 摘要原文：「there is a lack of information regarding dog size, breed, training status, the type of leash used at the time of injury」。
- Europe PMC 能查到的伸縮繩文獻只有眼外傷個案（Eye 1994、2012 的來信），講的是繩子回彈打傷眼睛，和摔倒無關，沒收。
- 所以標題只寫「繩子收短」，依據是手外科那篇的「shorter leash length」建議；備註寫「伸縮繩放長了就是長繩」，並寫明沒有直接比較的研究。

## 6. 骨質疏鬆的老人別牽大狗
- 任務原稿有這一句。來源裡只有 Pirruccio 建議「正在考慮養狗的人挑小一點的狗」，以及手外科那篇「With larger dogs especially」收短繩。沒有來源把骨質疏鬆和狗的大小連起來，所以標題沒寫「骨質疏鬆別牽大狗」，只在備註寫了挑小狗的建議，骨質疏鬆改成指路到第 1 節第 39、40 條。

## 證據等級和收益量級
- 證據等級 B：受傷數字是全國推算，可以量化；但三個動作（握在手心、收短、訓練狗）都只是研究作者和美國獸醫協會的建議，沒有研究算過做了能少摔多少。
- 收益量級憑判斷定「小」：口徑是死亡率（含骨折），閾值看相對降幅，這裡沒有任何降幅數據，只有受傷計數；按「只有替代終點」那一檔定小。
- 受益人：第 ② 檔，父母和祖父母。
