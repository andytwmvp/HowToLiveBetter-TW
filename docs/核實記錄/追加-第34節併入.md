### Smilkstein 1988 NEJM

Europe PMC 題錄：NEJM 319(24):1557-1562，DOI 10.1056/nejm198812153192401，PMID 3059186。摘要原文：

> Hepatotoxicity developed in 6.1 percent of patients at probable risk when N-acetylcysteine was started within 10 hours of acetaminophen ingestion and in 26.4 percent of such patients when therapy was begun 10 to 24 hours after ingestion.

> When given within eight hours of acetaminophen ingestion, N-acetylcysteine was protective regardless of the initial plasma acetaminophen concentration.

> We describe the outcomes of 2540 patients with acetaminophen ingestions treated with a loading dose of 140 mg of oral N-acetylcysteine …

> Among patients at high risk who were treated 16 to 24 hours after an acetaminophen overdose, hepatotoxicity developed in 41 percent--a rate lower than that among historical controls.

「probable risk」譯成「按血藥濃度判斷可能傷肝的病人」（原文按初始血藥濃度分檔）。研究沒有隨機分組，只和歷史病例比，寫進了備註；證據等級維持 B。

### FDA 問答：嬰兒液體對乙酰氨基酚的濃度變化

https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/questions-and-answers-important-change-concentration-over-counter-otc-liquid-acetaminophen-marketed

> Single ingredient OTC liquid acetaminophen marketed for infants had been available in 80 mg/0.8 mL and 80 mg/mL concentrations, while the single ingredient OTC liquid acetaminophen marketed for children had been available in a 160 mg/5 mL concentration. Having two very different concentrations of liquid acetaminophen on the market increased the likelihood for dosing confusion and medication errors involving unintentional overdoses in children.

> Do not mix and match dosing devices. … Only the dosing device provided with the product purchased to measure that product should be used to measure that product.

> Q6. Can dosing devices from the old and new concentrations of liquid acetaminophen marketed for infants be interchanged? A. No. Dosing devices sold with different products should never be interchanged.

「約 3 倍」是按這兩個濃度自算的：80 mg/0.8 mL = 100 mg/mL，160 mg/5 mL = 32 mg/mL，100 ÷ 32 ≈ 3.1。原 FDA 2011 年安全通告頁（…addition-another-concentration-liquid-acetaminophen-marketed-infants）已 404，改引這份問答頁。

MedlinePlus 同一頁也寫了：

> Always use the measuring cup or syringe provided by the manufacturer to measure each dose of the solution or suspension. Do not switch dosing devices between different products; always use the device that comes in the product packaging.

### Falagas 2010（家用茶匙容量）

Int J Clin Pract 64(9):1185-1189，DOI 10.1111/j.1742-1241.2010.02402.x，PMID 20653796。摘要原文：

> A total of 71 teaspoons and 49 tablespoons were provided by the 25 women … When these utensils were filled with water, the volume capacity of the 71 teaspoons and 49 tablespoons ranged from 2.5 to 7.3 ml (the mean and the median volume was 4.4 ml).

注意 2.5 到 7.3 毫升這個範圍是 71 把茶匙和 49 把湯匙合起來的，條目照此寫成「71 把茶匙和 49 把湯匙，裝滿水後容量從 2.5 毫升到 7.3 毫升不等」，沒有說成只是茶匙。

### Yin 2014 Pediatrics（茶匙、湯匙單位與量錯）

Pediatrics 134(2):e354-61，DOI 10.1542/peds.2014-0395，PMID 25022742。摘要原文：

> Compared with parents who used milliliter-only, parents who used teaspoon or tablespoon units had twice the odds of making an error with the intended (42.5% vs 27.6%, P = .02; adjusted odds ratio=2.3; 95% confidence interval, 1.2-4.4) …

> Furthermore, 16.7% used a nonstandard instrument. … Nonstandard instrument use partially mediated teaspoon and tablespoon-associated measurement errors.

研究對象是 2 家急診的 287 名家長。條目只寫了「劑量單位用茶匙、湯匙的家長」，沒寫成「用家裡勺子的家長」，因為原文比的是單位。
