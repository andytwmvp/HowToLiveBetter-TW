# 追加：第 20 節第 15 條，給嬰兒添置東西時別買帶輪子的學步車，想讓他坐著玩就換成沒輪子的遊戲站臺或遊戲圍欄

2026-10-14 追加在節末。全書原來沒有一處提到學步車（`grep -rn 學步車 book/ docs/` 無結果），不算重複。本節加完是 15 條，不到 20 條，按規則不補節首導覽。

和本節第 11 條（大件按「借、買二手、買新」的順序考慮）的分工：第 11 條講大件怎麼省錢和二手的安全邊界，這條講學步車這一樣東西壓根別買，備註裡指回第 11 條。

## 收益量級與證據等級

- 證據等級定 B：傷害數字來自美國全國急診傷害監測（NEISS）的大樣本，但只是記錄受傷人數，沒有「用學步車的孩子和不用的孩子相比風險高多少」的對照數字。推遲走路那一半更弱，只有橫斷面調查和質量偏低的小綜述。
- 收益量級定「中」，憑判斷：死亡率口徑按閾值要看相對降幅，這裡沒有降幅數字。學步車相關的傷害只要不用學步車就全部避開，住院孩子裡近四成是顱骨骨折，後果重；但死亡數低（美國 25 年報告 34 起），基礎發生率不高，所以不定「大」。
- 沒標爭議：傷害那一半各方一致，美國兒科學會、加拿大政府立場相同。推遲走路那一半證據不硬，已在收益欄如實寫出。

## 1. Sims 2018 Pediatrics
- 題錄：Sims A, Chounthirath T, Yang J, Hodges NL, Smith GA (2018). Infant Walker-Related Injuries in the United States. Pediatrics, 142(4), e20174332。PMID 30224365，<https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2017-4332>。作者、卷期經 Europe PMC REST（resultType=core）核對，摘要從同一接口取得。
- 約 23 萬：「An estimated 230 676 children <15 months old were treated for infant walker-related injuries in US emergency departments from 1990 to 2014.」
- 頭頸與樓梯：「Most of the children sustained head or neck injuries (90.6%) and 74.1% were injured by falling down the stairs in an infant walker.」
- 住院與顱骨骨折：「Among patients who were admitted to the hospital (4.5%), 37.8% had a skull fracture.」
- 強制標準之後：「The average annual number of injuries decreased by 22.7% (P = .019) during the 4-year period after the implementation of the federal mandatory safety standard compared with the 4-year period before the standard.」標準實施年份見結論「the federal mandatory safety standard in 2010」。
- 仍可避免：「Despite the decline in injuries, infant walkers remain an important and preventable source of injury among young children, which supports the American Academy of Pediatrics' call for a ban on their manufacture and sale in the United States.」條目寫成「仍是孩子受傷的一個重要來源，而且是可以避免的」，沒寫「完全」。

## 2. 美國兒科學會 2001 年立場聲明
- 題錄：American Academy of Pediatrics, Committee on Injury and Poison Prevention (2001). Injuries associated with infant walkers. Pediatrics, 108(3), 790-792。PMID 11533353，<https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.108.3.790>。摘要經 Europe PMC 取得。
- 死亡數：「Thirty-four infant walker-related deaths were reported from 1973 through 1998.」
- 防不住：「The use of warning labels, public education, adult supervision during walker use, and stair gates have all been demonstrated to be insufficient strategies to prevent injuries associated with infant walkers.」
- 1997 年標準：「walkers manufactured after June 30, 1997, must be wider than a 36-in doorway or must have a braking mechanism designed to stop the walker if 1 or more wheels drop off the riding surface, such as at the top of a stairway.」
- 主張禁售：「because there is no clear benefit from their use, the American Academy of Pediatrics recommends a ban on the manufacture and sale of mobile infant walkers.」
- 替代：「Stationary activity centers should be promoted as a safer alternative to mobile infant walkers.」
- 學走路：「Walkers do not help a child learn to walk; indeed, they can delay normal motor and mental development.」
- 摘要裡還有「In 1999, an estimated 8800 children younger than 15 months were treated in hospital emergency departments」，條目改用 Sims 2018 的 25 年總數，沒寫這一年的數。

## 3. 美國兒科學會家長頁 HealthyChildren.org
- <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/at-home/Pages/baby-walkers-a-dangerous-choice.aspx>，頁面寫「Last Updated 8/15/2022」。curl 帶代理取得。
- 大人在旁邊：「Most walker injuries happen while adults are watching. Parents or caregivers simply cannot respond quickly enough. A child in a walker can move more than 3 feet in 1 second!」3 英尺約 0.91 米，條目寫成「3 英尺（約 0.9 米）以上」，說人話寫「0.9 米以上」。
- 不能學走路：「Many parents think walkers will help their children learn to walk. But they don't. In fact, walkers can actually delay when a child starts to walk.」
- 燙傷溺水：「It is now easier for a child to pull a tablecloth off a table and spill hot coffee, grab pot handles off the stove」；「Drown —child can fall into a pool or bathtub while in a walker.」
- 替代品三樣：「Stationary activity centers —they look like walkers but have no wheels. They usually have seats that rotate, tip, and bounce. Play yards or playpens ... High chairs」。
- 別人家和托育機構：「be sure that there are no walkers wherever your child is being cared for, such as child care centers or in someone else's home.」

## 4. Garrett 2002 BMJ
- 題錄：Garrett M, McElroy AM, Staines A (2002). Locomotor milestones and babywalkers: cross sectional study. BMJ, 324(7352), 1494。PMID 12077035，PMC116446，<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.324.7352.1494>。Europe PMC 沒有摘要，全文從 <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC116446/> 取得。
- 設計和人數：「We performed a cross sectional survey of normal healthy infants born at term attending day care centres」；「We excluded 17 premature infants leaving 190 valid responses」。愛爾蘭都柏林大學的研究，樣本來自北愛爾蘭 Foyle 地區托育中心。
- 獨立走路：表中「Walk alone 58.14 61.12 −2.98 (−4.51 to −1.45) 0.0002」，單位是周，不用學步車的 58.14 周，用的 61.12 周。條目寫「平均晚約 3 周」。
- 劑量關係：「each aggregated 24 hours of babywalker use was associated with a delay of 3.3 (95% confidence interval 2.5 to 4.1) days in walking alone」。條目只寫「用得越多晚得越多」。
- 同刊有一封來信（Gardner FE 2002, BMJ 325:657，題為「Potential confounding factors were not measured」）質疑沒測混雜因素，條目用「只是一次性問卷調查，沒有分組對照」交代這層弱點。

## 5. Burrows & Griffiths 2002 小綜述
- 題錄：Burrows P, Griffiths P (2002). Do baby walkers delay onset of walking in young children? Br J Community Nurs, 7(11), 581-586。PMID 12447120，<https://doi.org/10.12968/bjcn.2002.7.11.10889>。摘要經 Europe PMC 取得。
- 「Two RCTs and two cohort studies were identified」；「The results of the two RCTs did not demonstrate a significant effect on the onset of walking.」
- 「a pooled analysis of the four studies suggested a delay of between 11 and 26 days. Although the quality of the studies was relatively poor these studies lend no support to the argument that walkers aid the development of walking.」
- 另查到 Badihian 2017 系統綜述（Iran J Child Neurol 11(4):1-6，PMID 29201117），結論是「Evidence against baby walker is not enough regarding its negative effect on child development」，和條目「這一半的證據不硬」一致，沒進來源欄。Siegel & Burton 1999（J Dev Behav Pediatr 20(5):355-361，109 名嬰兒，用學步車的坐、爬、走都更晚）同樣只是跟蹤比較，結論方向和 Garrett 相同，沒另引。

## 6. 加拿大禁售
- 新聞稿：Health Canada News Release 2004-15，<https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2004/04/minister-pettigrew-announces-ban-baby-walkers.html>。curl 超時，無頭 Chrome 帶代理取得。
  - 「April 7, 2004 ... Health Minister Pierre Pettigrew today announced the Government of Canada's immediate prohibition of the sale, advertisement and importation of baby walkers in Canada.」
  - 「This prohibition also applies to the sale of baby walkers as second-hand items and so they may not be sold at flea markets or garage sales. Persons who already have baby walkers are advised to permanently dismantle and dispose of them.」
  - 新聞稿裡部長說「Canada is the first country in the world to ban the sale of these products」，條目沒寫「全世界第一個」，只寫加拿大禁賣。
- 現行法：Canada Consumer Product Safety Act (S.C. 2010, c. 21)，<https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-1.68/FullText.html>，curl 帶代理取得。
  - 第 5 條：「No person shall manufacture, import, advertise or sell a consumer product listed in Schedule 2.」
  - 附表 2 第 3 項：「Baby walkers that are mounted on wheels or on any other device permitting movement of the walker and that have an enclosed area supporting the baby in a sitting or standing position so that their feet touch the floor, thereby enabling the horizontal movement of the walker.」
