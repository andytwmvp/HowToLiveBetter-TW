# 追加：第 16 節第 13、14 條（慢性腎病篩查、結腸癌術後運動）

2026-10-14。主任務列的兩個候選，追加在第 16 節末尾，沒插中間。本節加完 14 條，不到 20 條，不補導覽。

## 查重與四問
- 全書 grep「尿白蛋白」「腎小球」「肌酐」「慢性腎病」「KDIGO」：沒有講腎病篩查的條目。只有第 1 節 PrEP 條提到開藥前查腎功能，第 9 節賣腎，都不是同一件事。
- 是否並進第 7 條（查眼底和腳）：沒並。第 7 條標題只講糖尿病的眼和腳，往裡加腎要改標題，而腎病篩查還管高血壓病人，人群不同。所以單開第 13 條，第 7 條備註末尾加了一句指路。第 3 條（複查記本子）講的是記錄方式，不是查哪一項，也沒並。
- 全書 grep「結腸癌」「結直腸癌」「CHALLENGE」「Courneya」：只有第 1 節第 19 條結直腸癌篩查，和第 16 節第 4 條備註裡提到結直腸癌病人用偏方，都不是術後運動。
- 四問：① 中國每年結直腸癌新發病人多，慢性腎病約 8200 萬人，都是普通讀者會碰上的；② 知曉率只有 10%，糖尿病病人裡查過的只有 27%；術後多數人以為該靜養，不知道有隨機試驗支持運動；③ 動作是新的；④ 都有原始文獻。

## 第 13 條 慢性腎病篩查

### Wang L 2023, JAMA Internal Medicine（第六次中國慢性病及危險因素監測）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2022.6817>，摘要從 Europe PMC 取（PMC9941971）。
- 「of the 176 874 adults 18 years and older with measurements of eGFR and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio in 2018 to 2019」
- 「The estimated prevalence of CKD, impaired kidney function, and albuminuria were 8.2%, 2.2%, and 6.7%, respectively.」
- 「Among the adults with CKD, 73.3%, 25.0%, and 1.8% were at stage 1 to 2, 3, and 4 to 5, respectively, and the awareness of CKD was 10.0%.」
- 「This cross-sectional study found a weighted estimated of 82 million adults with CKD in China in 2018 to 2019.」
- 主任務點名的 Zhang L 2012 Lancet（doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(12)60033-6）：摘要裡有患病率 10.8%、約 1.195 億人，但知曉率不在摘要裡，Lancet 全文頁本機取不到（只返回約 5.8 KB 的殼頁）。所以改用更新的 2018–2019 全國監測，Zhang 2012 沒引。

### Shi L 2024, JMIR Public Health and Surveillance（IMPROVE 研究）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.2196/54429>（PMC11399742）
- 「A national study from December 2021 to September 2022 enlisted 9672 participants with type 2 diabetes from 45 hospitals that had endocrinology departments.」
- 「The prevalence, screening, and awareness rates of CKD among patients with type 2 diabetes were 31% (2997/9672), 27% (810/2997), and 54.8% (5295/9672), respectively.」
- 正文寫成「這 2997 人裡，以前查過腎病的只有 27%」，取的是 810/2997 這個分母。知曉率 54.8% 的分母是全部糖尿病病人，口徑不同，沒寫進正文。

### KDIGO 2024 CKD 指南
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.kint.2023.10.018>，Crossref 核到 Kidney International 105(4) S117–S314。全文 PDF 從 kdigo.org 下載（KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf），英文 pdftotext 能逐字取。
- 「Practice Point 1.1.1.1: Test people at risk for and with chronic kidney disease (CKD) using both urine albumin measurement and assessment of glomerular filtration rate (GFR).」
- 「Practice Point 1.1.1.2: Following incidental detection of elevated urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR), hematuria, or low estimated GFR (eGFR), repeat tests to confirm presence of CKD.」
- 「The highest priority conditions for CKD detection are hypertension, diabetes, and CVD, including heart failure. For diabetes, the ADA and KDIGO recommend annual screening of people with diabetes for CKD.」
- 高血壓病人的篩查間隔，全文沒有找到固定頻率，所以標題寫「有高血壓的也要查」，沒寫「每年」。

### ADA Standards of Care 2026, Section 11
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S011>，Diabetes Care 49(Suppl 1) S246–S260（Crossref）。全文從 PMC 頁面取（PMC12690176），Europe PMC 的 fullTextXML 接口返回 500。
- 「11.1a Assess kidney function with random urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) and estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) at least annually in people with type 1 diabetes with duration of ≥5 years and in all people with type 2 diabetes regardless of treatment. B」
- 「11.6a In nonpregnant people with diabetes and hypertension, either an ACE inhibitor or an angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB) is recommended for those with moderately increased albuminuria (UACR 30–299 mg/g creatinine) B and is strongly recommended for those with severely increased albuminuria (UACR ≥300 mg/g creatinine) and/or eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 ...」

### Heerspink 2020 NEJM（DAPA-CKD）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2024816>，摘要從 Europe PMC 取。
- 「We randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 ... and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio ... of 200 to 5000」
- 「Over a median of 2.4 years, a primary outcome event occurred in 197 of 2152 participants (9.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 312 of 2152 participants (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.51 to 0.72」
- 「Death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.69; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.53 to 0.88; P = 0.004).」
- 「The effects of dapagliflozin were similar in participants with type 2 diabetes and in those without type 2 diabetes.」

### 國家基本公共衛生服務規範
- 主任務要求以原文為準。gov.cn 政策庫用 `searchfield=title` 搜「基本公共衛生服務規範」無結果；衛健委官網的第三版規範是中文 PDF，本機 pdftotext 取不出中文。核不到原文，正文不寫「基本公衛免費查不查這一項」。

### 定級
- 證據等級 B：篩查本身沒有隨機試驗比過死亡；少死的數字來自已經查出尿蛋白偏高、再吃藥的人。
- 收益量級「中」憑判斷：收益欄裡死亡 HR 0.69 機械套是「大」，但那是查出來以後吃藥這一步的效果，只落在查出異常的那部分人身上，所以降一檔。
- 成本「錢=少」：一次尿白蛋白肌酐比加血肌酐，屬於常規化驗，正文沒寫具體價格（各地收費不同，沒查到統一官方價）。

## 第 14 條 結腸癌術後運動

### Courneya 2025 NEJM（CHALLENGE，CCTG CO.21）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2502760>，393(1):13–25，摘要從 Europe PMC 取（PMID 40450658）。NEJM 全文頁本機取不到。
- 「In this phase 3, randomized trial conducted at 55 centers, we assigned patients with resected colon cancer who had completed adjuvant chemotherapy to participate in a structured exercise program (exercise group) or to receive health-education materials alone (health-education group) over a 3-year period. The primary end point was disease-free survival.」
- 「a total of 889 patients underwent randomization to the exercise group (445 patients) or the health-education group (444 patients). At a median follow-up of 7.9 years, disease-free survival was significantly longer in the exercise group than in the health-education group (hazard ratio for disease recurrence, new primary cancer, or death, 0.72; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.55 to 0.94; P = 0.02). The 5-year disease-free survival was 80.3% in the exercise group and 73.9% in the health-education group」
- 「Results support longer overall survival in the exercise group than in the health-education group (hazard ratio for death, 0.63; 95% CI, 0.43 to 0.94). The 8-year overall survival was 90.3% in the exercise group and 83.2% in the health-education group」
- 「Musculoskeletal adverse events occurred more often in the exercise group than in the health-education group (in 18.5% vs. 11.5% of patients).」
- 「... resulted in significantly longer disease-free survival and findings consistent with longer overall survival.」備註「總死亡是次要結果……和活得更長相符」據此寫。

### ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00819208（方案與入組條件，API v2 取）
- 入組：「Completely resected histologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the colon」「High-risk stage II disease ...」「Stage III disease ...」「Chemotherapy must have been completed (i.e. last dose received) a minimum of 60 days and a maximum of 180 days prior to registration.」「Current physical activity levels do not meet the recommended guidelines (≥ 150 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous or ≥ 75 minutes of vigorous physical activity/week)」「these imaging tests must not show evidence of metastatic or locally-recurrent colon cancer」「No rectal cancer」；入組前要做次極量運動測試或 6 分鐘步行測試。
- 幹預：「Part I (intensive intervention for 6 months): Patients undergo 12 mandatory biweekly face-to-face behavior support sessions combined with 12 mandatory supervised physical activity sessions to increase their physical activity goal by 10 metabolic equivalent task (MET) hours/week.」「Part II (reduced intervention for months 6-12): Patients undergo 12 mandatory biweekly face-to-face or telephone behavior support sessions ...」「Phase 3: Minimal intervention for months 12-36」
- 「Most patients are likely to choose a walking program however any PA of at least moderate intensity level is acceptable.」正文寫成「方案預計多數人會選走路」，這是方案裡的預期，不是結果。

### Courneya 2016 CEBP（一年可行性結果）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-15-1267>
- 「The SEP group reported an increase in recreational PA of 15.6 MET-hours/week compared with 5.1 MET-hours/week in the HEM group」

### Chan 2026 JCO（費用分析）
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-00765>，44(22):2130–2139（Crossref）。
- 「despite an up-front cost of $2,917 (CAD)/participant to provide the SEP, the SEP strategy (total cost: $31,957 (CAD)/participant) was dominant over HEM (total cost: $33,546 (CAD)/participant), being less costly (-$1,589/participant)」；基線是加拿大公共支付方角度、5 年。

### 定級
- 證據等級 A：大型多中心隨機試驗，有具體 HR。只有一項試驗，但沒找到反方試驗，沒標爭議。
- 收益量級「大」：無病生存降約 28%、死亡降約 37%，按死亡率口徑 ≥20% 機械套。
- 成本「錢=少」：國內沒有同款成套項目，按醫院運動處方或康復科評估的掛號檢查費算；試驗裡的 2917 加元是加拿大整套指導費用，沒換算成人民幣寫進成本欄。「時間=中」：每週多出 10 個代謝當量小時的運動，是每週小時級。「毅力=是」：要堅持三年。
- 主任務提的「其他癌種的觀察性證據」沒寫：不在本條動作範圍內，備註只寫了「不能直接套到別的癌症和晚期病人身上」。
