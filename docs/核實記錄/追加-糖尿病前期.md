# 追加：第 2 節第 45 條，查出糖尿病前期就靠少吃、減重、多動把糖尿病往後推

2026-10-14。和第 1 節第 8 條（35 歲以後超重就查空腹血糖）分工：那一條管怎麼查出來，這一條管查出來以後做什麼。追加在第 2 節末尾，條號先佔 45。全書原來只有第 1 節第 8 條備註裡一句「相當一部分人光靠改生活方式就能把它扭回去」，沒有數字，不算重複。

## 證據等級與收益量級
- 證據等級 A：三項隨機分組試驗（大慶、美國 DPP、芬蘭 DPS），都能給出具體的風險比。
- 標「爭議」：只有大慶看到總死亡率下降。DPP 跟到中位 21 年總死亡 HR 1.02 不顯著，DPS 跟到平均 10.6 年總死亡 HR 0.57（0.21–1.58）不顯著。這兩項作為反方寫進收益欄，來源欄標「爭議方」。
- 收益量級「大」：按死亡率口徑機械套，大慶 30 年總死亡 HR 0.74，相對降約 26%，過了 20% 的線。備註裡寫明美國和芬蘭沒有重複出來。
- 成本標籤：錢=0；時間=多，和本節第 11 條、第 32 條一致（每天都要動、每頓都要管）；毅力=是。
- 人群標籤：不加。糖尿病前期是一種身體狀況，不在 7 個值裡，按規則同慢病一樣不加。
- 標題寫的是「把糖尿病往後推」而不是「扭回去」：三項試驗的主要終點都是得不得糖尿病、什麼時候得，沒有一項以「血糖回到正常」為主要終點。

## 1. Pan XR 等 (1997)，大慶研究原始報告
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.2337/diacare.20.4.537>（Europe PMC 摘要，PMID 9096977）
- 原文：「Of these individuals, 577 were classified (using World Health Organization criteria) as having IGT. Subjects were randomized by clinic into a clinical trial, either to a control group or to one of three active treatment groups: diet only, exercise only, or diet plus exercise.」
- 原文：「The cumulative incidence of diabetes at 6 years was 67.7% (95% CI, 59.8-75.2) in the control group compared with 43.8% (95% CI, 35.5-52.3) in the diet group, 41.1% (95% CI, 33.4-49.4) in the exercise group, and 46.0% (95% CI, 37.3-54.7) in the diet-plus-exercise group (P < 0.05).」
- 原文：「The relative decrease in rate of development of diabetes in the active treatment groups was similar when subjects were stratified as lean or overweight (BMI < or > or = 25 kg/m2).」
- 原文：「In a proportional hazards analysis adjusted for differences in baseline BMI and fasting glucose, the diet, exercise, and diet-plus-exercise interventions were associated with 31% (P < 0.03), 46% (P < 0.0005), and 42% (P < 0.005) reductions in risk of developing diabetes, respectively.」

## 3. Gong Q 等 (2019)，大慶 30 年隨訪
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(19)30093-2>；全文在 PMC8172050（<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8172050/>，帶代理取到；Europe PMC 的 fullTextXML 接口返回 500）
- 原文：「compared with control, the combined intervention group had a median delay in diabetes onset of 3·96 years (95% CI 1·25 to 6·67; p=0·0042), fewer cardiovascular disease events (hazard ratio 0·74, 95% CI 0·59-0·92; p=0·0060), a lower incidence of microvascular complications (0·65, 0·45-0·95; p=0·025), fewer cardiovascular disease deaths (0·67, 0·48-0·94; p=0·022), fewer all-cause deaths (0·74, 0·61-0·89; p=0·0015), and an average increase in life expectancy of 1·44 years (95% CI 0·20-2·68; p=0·023).」
- 小血管併發症的定義（正文方法部分）：「The primary outcome of composite microvascular disease was an aggregate outcome of retinopathy, defined as a history of photocoagulation, blindness from retinal disease, or proliferative retinopathy; nephropathy, defined as a history of end-stage renal disease, renal dialysis, renal transplantation, or death from chronic kidney disease; and neuropathy, defined as a history of leg, ankle, or foot ulceration, gangrene, or amputation.」都是重的結局（失明、透析、腿腳潰爛截肢一類），所以條目寫「眼底、腎、神經的嚴重小血管併發症」。
- 侷限（正文討論部分）：「First, the sample size is small because the original trial was designed to study diabetes incidence and not complications.」
- 體重（正文討論部分）：「[DPS 和 DPP] were done in people with impaired glucose tolerance with an average BMI greater than 30 kg/m2」；「In the Da Qing study, the baseline mean BMI of participants was only 25.7 kg/m2, and weight loss was encouraged only in those participants with a BMI higher than 25 kg/m2. The interventions resulted in only small changes in BMI, suggesting that the reduced diabetes incidence in the intervention group was mainly attributable to factors such as changes in dietary composition and increased physical activity rather than weight loss.」備註裡「不胖的人也照樣要改吃法、多動」出自這一段和 Pan 1997 的分層結果。

## 10. 健康中國行動（2019—2030年），（十四）糖尿病防治行動
- URL：<https://wjw.hunan.gov.cn/wjw/ztzl/knowledge/tzggzcfg/202003/t20200303_11359594.html>（湖南省衛健委轉載，2026-10-14 直連 200；第 2 節第 12 條用的是同一個頁面）。gov.cn 原文頁 content_5409694 本輪取到的是空殼，沒用。
- 定義：「6.1mmol/L≤空腹血糖(FBG)<7.0mmol/L,或7.8mmol/L≤糖負荷2小時血糖(2hPG)<11.1mmol/L,則為糖調節受損,也稱糖尿病前期,屬於糖尿病的極高危人群。」
- 做法：「糖尿病前期人群可通過飲食控制和科學運動降低發病風險,建議每半年檢測1次空腹血糖或餐後2小時血糖。同時密切關注其他心腦血管危險因素,並給予適當的幹預措施。建議超重或肥胖者使體重指數(BMI)達到或接近24kg/㎡,或體重至少下降7%,每日飲食總熱量至少減少400~500kcal,飽和脂肪酸攝入佔總脂肪酸攝入的30%以下,中等強度體力活動至少保持在150分鐘/周。」
- 飽和脂肪酸那半句原文寫的是「佔總脂肪酸攝入的30%以下」，口徑和常見寫法（佔總能量的百分比）不同，讀者難照做，條目沒收。
