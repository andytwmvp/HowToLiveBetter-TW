# 追加：第 1 節第 42 條，裝好護罩、戴上護目鏡加面屏再用角磨機

2026-10-04。來自 issue #76（讀者提議角磨機安全做法）。全書原來沒有電動工具條目，第 19 節第 10 條講的是粉塵、噪聲、化學毒物的防護，不算重複。追加在第 1 節末尾，沒有插在中間；節首導覽掛進「家裡的火、氣、電」一塊，短名「裝好護罩再用角磨機」。角磨機多是家裡裝修、自己動手時用，和滅火器、燃氣軟管同屬家裡的器具風險，所以沒放進「孩子、老人和野外的意外」。

## 和讀者原稿的差別
- 原稿「登高作業」一段（HSE work at height）按事先定的刪掉，不寫。
- 原稿的動作逐條在 CCOHS 兩頁和昆士蘭通報裡核到了，見下。
- 補了一條原稿沒有的動作：別裝木工鋸片切木頭。國內能找到的兩份官方事故調查報告（哈爾濱 2019、深圳福田 2024）死因都是這個，日本一項病例研究也專門講它，比原稿裡任何一條都更貼近國內實際出事的樣子，所以寫進了標題。
- 原稿沒有傷害數字。補了韓國急診監測、四川綿陽眼外傷、土耳其手外傷、日本斷指四項病例數據，只用來說明傷得多重。
- 證據等級按 C：全是指南和事故調查，沒有研究算過照做能少傷多少人。

## 1. CCOHS, Abrasive Wheels - Use of Portable Grinders（2025-06-23 修訂）
- URL：<https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/abrasive_wheels/safeuse1.html>（2026-10-04 帶代理取到）
- 護罩：「Always use the provided guards and make sure they are adjusted properly as per the manufacturer's manual. Do not use the grinder if there are missing or damaged guards.」
- 檢查：「Inspect the tool and attachments for damage before each use.」；「Do not use wheels or attachments that are cracked, damaged, or that excessively vibrate.」
- 轉速：「Check that the speed marked on the wheel or attachment is equal to or greater than the grinder's maximum speed.」；「Do not use a wheel or attachment with a maximum RPM lower than the RPM rating of the grinder.」
- 尺寸：「Do not force wheels or attachments onto a grinder that is the wrong size or change mounting hole sizes.」
- 護目：「Wear safety glasses or goggles, or a face shield (with safety glasses or goggles) to protect against flying particles.」
- 雙手站穩：「Use both hands when holding the grinder. Maintain a firm grip on both handles and ensure secure footing during operation.」
- 固定工件：「Make sure the item or material you are grinding is secured.」
- 火花與易燃物：「Direct sparks and debris away from yourself and others.」；「Make sure the work area is clear of flammable products」；「Do not use grinders near flammable materials.」
- 停轉再放：「Do not put the grinder on the floor or working surface until the wheel or attachment has stopped turning.」
- 衣物：「Do not wear loose clothing or dangling jewellery, as they may get caught in the grinder's moving parts. If you have long hair, keep it tied back.」

## 2. CCOHS, Powered Hand Tools - Use of Grinder Attachments（2025-04-15）
- URL：<https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/power_tools/powered-hand-tools-use-of-grinder-attachments.html>
- 換片斷電：「Disconnect the grinder from the power source before installing the cutting disc.」
- 檢查片子：「Inspect the disc for damage before use, such as cracks, chips, or warping. Do not use damaged discs as they may shatter during operation, causing serious injury.」
- 護罩：「Ensure the wheel guard is in place while operating the grinder.」
- 易燃物：「Direct sparks and debris away from yourself and others. Keep flammable materials away from the cutting area.」
- 這一頁寫的是「tight-fitting gloves」，可以戴貼手的手套，所以條目只寫寬鬆衣物收好，沒有寫「不許戴手套」。

## 3. Workplace Health and Safety Queensland, Guards and discs on angle grinders（2016-03-04 發，2017-11-20 更新）
- URL：<https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/news-and-events/alerts/workplace-health-and-safety-alerts/2017/guards-and-discs-on-angle-grinders>
- 死亡案例：「In February 2016, a worker was killed while operating a 9 inch (230 mm) angle grinder when part of a broken disc struck his chest. It appears that the high impact of the projectile caused fatal internal injuries.」
- 速度：「Cutting discs are usually designed for a maximum outside edge speed (i.e. peripheral speed) of approximately 70 metres/second」；「Fitting a 14 inch (356 mm) cut-off disc to a 9 inch (230 mm) angle grinder will increase the edge speed excessively to at least 120 m/s」
- 動作：「Use an angle grinder with the correct guard supplied by the manufacturer.」；「Fit the right sized disc that has a hole matching the spindle flange.」；「Ensure the rated speed marked on the disc is not less than the rated speed marked on the angle grinder.」；「Hold the angle grinder with both hands」；「Use a grinding disc for grinding. Grinding with a cutting disc damages the disc and increases the likelihood of the disc breaking.」；「Ensure damaged discs are thrown out and not re-used.」
- 讀者原稿「切割片不能用側面打磨」對應的是這一句。原文只說切割片不能拿來打磨，沒提「側面」，條目按原文寫。

## 4. 哈爾濱市松北區人民政府，三發裝卸運輸有限公司「8.28」機械傷害事故調查報告（2019）

## 6. Song J 等 (2023)，韓國急診傷害深度監測 2011–2018
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.20408/jti.2021.0057>（Europe PMC 摘要，PMID 39381671）
- 原文：「Among 8,697 ED visits, 4,603 patients had been using a grinder and 4,094 had been using a nongrinder tool. The most frequently injured body part while using a grinder was the hand (46.4%), followed by the head (23.0%).」；「using a grinder (OR, 2.073; 95% CI, 1.877-2.290)」。
- grinder 在這項研究裡指砂輪機一類工具，沒有單拆角磨機，所以條目寫「砂輪機這一類工具」。

## 沒收的內容和理由
- 晉中市應急管理局《角磨機使用方法及安全使用操作規程》（<https://yjglj.sxjz.gov.cn/yjgm/content_278833>）：正文開頭自稱轉自「賢集網」，屬於商業網站轉載；內容混著臺式砂輪機的規程（托架距砂輪 5 毫米、站在側面磨刀具），還寫「不準戴手套」，和 CCOHS 允許貼手手套衝突。沒引。
- GB/T 7442-2007《角向磨光機》：全國標準信息公共服務平臺能查到題錄，現行、2021 年複審繼續有效，但它是產品標準，不規定使用者怎麼操作，和條目的動作無關。沒引。
- GB 2494 固結磨具安全要求、GB/T 3883 系列的原文：沒有取到能逐字核對的官方全文，只用了福田報告轉引的那兩項。
- 北京通州應急管理局「角磨機防護罩不符合國家標準，罰」一文：鏈接返回 404。疏附縣應急管理局執法案例只有一句「角磨機人為拆除防護罩」，信息量不夠，沒引。
- Curran 2021 病例報告裡的「每年 5000 多起傷害」：摘要沒有說是哪個國家、哪份統計，核不了，沒寫。
- 澳大利亞 Med J Aust 2000、Injury 2000 等單病例或三例報告：只說明傷得重，已有的數據夠用，沒有再加。
- 「站在片子側面啟動」「新片空轉一分鐘」「片子有效期 3 年」這幾條 CCOHS 寫了，但讀者原稿沒提，條目篇幅有限，沒寫進去。

## 收益量級
- 定為「中」，憑判斷：後果可以是死亡、失明、斷指，但沒有任何來源給出照做之後的降幅，沒法按相對降幅機械套；暴露人群只限用角磨機的人，比第 1 條（安全帶）這類窄得多。和第 41 條（冰面）同樣處理。
