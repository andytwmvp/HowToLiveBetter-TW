## 1. Marjoribanks 2015，Cochrane CD001751.pub3
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001751.pub3>（PMID 26224322；題錄與摘要經 Europe PMC，作者串 Marjoribanks J, Ayeleke RO, Farquhar C, Proctor M，2015 年第 7 期）。doi.org 跳轉到 Wiley 被 Cloudflare 擋（403），按 Europe PMC 摘要核對。

- 原文：「NSAIDs were more effective for pain relief than placebo (OR 4.37, 95% CI 3.76 to 5.09; 35 RCTs, I(2) = 53%, low quality evidence). This suggests that if 18% of women taking placebo achieve moderate or excellent pain relief, between 45% and 53% taking NSAIDs will do so.」
- 原文：「overall adverse effects: OR 1.29, 95% CI 1.11 to 1.51 … gastrointestinal adverse effects: OR 1.58, 95% CI 1.12 to 2.23 … neurological adverse effects: OR 2.74, 95% CI 1.66 to 4.53 … if 10% of women taking placebo experience side effects, between 11% and 14% of women taking NSAIDs will do so.」

## 2. NICE NG73
- URL：<https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng73/chapter/Recommendations>（帶代理 curl 取到，頁面寫 Published 06 September 2017，Last updated 11 November 2024）
- 1.3.1：「Suspect endometriosis in women (including young women aged 17 and under) presenting with 1 or more of the following symptoms or signs: chronic pelvic pain; period-related pain (dysmenorrhoea) affecting daily activities and quality of life; deep pain during or after sexual intercourse; period-related or cyclical gastrointestinal symptoms, in particular, painful bowel movements; period-related or cyclical urinary symptoms, in particular, blood in the urine or pain passing urine; infertility in association with 1 or more of the above.」
- 1.4.2：「Consider a short trial (for example, 3 months) of paracetamol or a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) alone or in combination for first-line management of endometriosis-related pain.」
- 1.4.3：「If a trial of paracetamol or an NSAID (alone or in combination) does not provide adequate pain relief, consider other forms of pain management and referral for further assessment.」
- 1.5.4：「Do not exclude the possibility of endometriosis if the abdominal or pelvic examination and ultrasound scan are normal」。
- 標題裡的「吃三個月藥也壓不住」對應 1.4.2 和 1.4.3；「痛到耽誤上班上學」對應 1.3.1 的「affecting daily activities and quality of life」。

## 定級
- 證據 A：止痛效果來自 80 項隨機對照試驗的 Cochrane 彙總，有具體數字。作者把多數比較評為低質量證據，備註已寫明原因（方法報告不清、59% 藥廠資助）。不標爭議：沒有找到反方證據說這類藥對原發性痛經無效。
- 收益「中」，是判斷：Cochrane 只量了緩解比例，沒有量誤工時間，套不上時間口徑的閾值。憑的是 Schoep 的損失量級：缺勤 1.3 天加效率損失 8.9 天，每年約 10 天，按 8 小時工作日攤到每週約 1.6 小時，落在「每週小時級」。止痛藥只能挽回其中一部分，所以不往上定「大」。
- 成本標籤：錢=少（一盒布洛芬）、時間=少、毅力=否。去婦科那一步是有信號才做，不計入標籤。

## 沒寫、沒取到的
- 「止痛藥會上癮」「越吃越不管用」：沒找到針對痛經用這類藥的原始研究可以直接駁，條目不寫這句斷言，只用 Cochrane 的效果數字和第 34 節第 9 條（過度使用門檻管的是頭痛）回應。
- 「生了孩子就好了」：沒查到可逐字核對的原始文獻，沒寫。
- 中國的數據：子宮內膜異位症診治指南（第三版，中華婦產科雜誌 2021）原文沒取到，國內診斷延遲和痛經患病率都沒寫。
- 痛經時疼痛異常、同時月經推遲的宮外孕風險：本條範圍之外，沒寫。
