# 第 2 節來源核實記錄

核實方式：所有來源均通過 WebFetch 打開 Europe PMC REST 記錄（`<https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=DOI:<doi>&resultType=core&format=json`>，個別用 `TITLE:` 或 `EXT_ID:<pmid> AND SRC:MED` 查詢），記錄裡含題名、作者、期刊、年份、DOI、PMID 和完整摘要，引用的數字均在摘要裡。PubMed 網頁版對 WebFetch 返回 cookie 攔截頁，doi.org 返回 302 後出版商頁面（NEJM）返回 403，因此以 Europe PMC 記錄為準。DOI 一律按 Europe PMC 記錄填寫（其中 Aune 2016 堅果一文 DOI 實為 `10.1186/s12916-016-0730-3`，我原記憶的 `-0730-5` 有誤，該文最終未收入正文）。

## 第 42 條 植物油代替豬油黃油

- <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD011737.pub3> — 已確認：Hooper L 等, Cochrane 2020。摘要原文："15 randomised controlled trials (RCTs) (16 comparisons, 56,675 participants)"；"reduced the risk of combined cardiovascular events by 17% (risk ratio (RR) 0.83; 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.70 to 0.98"；all-cause mortality "RR 0.96; 95% CI 0.90 to 1.03"；cardiovascular mortality "RR 0.95; 95% CI 0.80 to 1.12"；"Subgrouping did not suggest significant differences between replacement of saturated fat calories with polyunsaturated fat or carbohydrate, and data on replacement with monounsaturated fat and protein was very limited"。
