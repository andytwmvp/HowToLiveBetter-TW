# 追加第 13 節第 45、46 條：孩子吞紐扣電池或磁珠，有人喝醉了

2026-10-14。兩條都追加在節末，掛進節首導覽「中毒、火災和意外」一塊。新增 8 處條目引用（節首 2 處，第 45 條 3 處，第 46 條 3 處），`check-refs --check` 從 1558 處漲到 1566 處。

## 查重

全書 grep「紐扣電池」「磁力珠」「磁珠」「巴克球」「強磁」：無。grep「醉酒」「喝醉」「酒精中毒」「同飲」：只有第 8 節第 7 條（酒駕）的成本、備註提到勸酒和醉酒，講的是開車，不是照看醉酒的人。第 2 節第 19 到 21 條講長期少喝、戒酒，第 34 節第 11 條講頭孢配酒，都不重複。第 16 條（抽搐側躺）和第 20 條（誤服清潔劑別催吐）動作相近，但對象不同，互相指路即可。

全書沒有「同飲人責任」的條目，所以第 46 條把法院案例寫進備註，沒有指到別節。

## 第 45 條：孩子吞了紐扣電池或兩顆以上強磁珠

### Litovitz 2010（Emerging battery-ingestion hazard）

<https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2009-3037>，Pediatrics 125(6):1168-1177，PMID 20498173，摘要取自 Europe PMC。

- 6.7 倍：「a 6.7-fold increase in the percentage of button battery ingestions with major or fatal outcomes from 1985 to 2009 (National Poison Data System)」
- 20 毫米鋰電池、4 歲以下：「Outcomes were significantly worse for large-diameter lithium cells (> or = 20 mm) and children who were younger than 4 years. The 20-mm lithium cell was implicated in most severe outcomes.」
- 2 到 2.5 小時：「Severe burns with sequelae occurred in just 2 to 2.5 hours.」
- 92%、56%：「Most fatal (92%) or major outcome (56%) ingestions were not witnessed.」
- 27%、54%：「At least 27% of major outcome and 54% of fatal cases were misdiagnosed, usually because of nonspecific presentations.」
- 取出後繼續加重：「Injuries extended after removal, with unanticipated and delayed esophageal perforations, tracheoesophageal fistulas, fistulization into major vessels, and massive hemorrhage.」

### Litovitz 2010（Preventing battery ingestions: an analysis of 8648 cases）

<https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2009-3038>，Pediatrics 125(6):1178-1183，PMID 20498172。

- 61.8%：「Batteries that were ingested by children who were younger than 6 years were most often obtained directly from a product (61.8%)」
- 36.3%：「Batteries that were intended for hearing aids were implicated in 36.3% of ingestions.」（這是全部病例的比例，不限老人，條目裡寫成「全部病例裡」）
- 15.5%：「Batteries were mistaken for pills in 15.5% of ingestions, mostly by older adults.」

### 美國國家中毒控制中心（National Capital Poison Center）處置指南

<https://www.poison.org/battery/guideline>，2026-10-14 帶代理 curl 取全文。

- 「If battery ingestion is suspected: Do not induce vomiting.」
- 「Batteries lodged in the esophagus may cause serious burns in as little as 2 hours. Do not wait for symptoms to develop. Patients with a battery in the esophagus may be asymptomatic initially.」
- 「If the patient is ≤ 12 years, immediately obtain an x-ray to locate the battery.」
- 蜂蜜：「Administer honey immediately and while en route to the ER, if: A lithium coin cell may have been ingested ...; The child is 12 months of age or older (because honey is not safe in children younger than one year); The battery was swallowed within the prior 12 hours ...; The child is able to swallow; and Honey is immediately available. Give 10 mL (2 teaspoons) of honey by mouth every 10 minutes for up to 6 doses.」
- 「Honey is NOT a substitute for immediate removal of a battery lodged in the esophagus. Honey slows the development of battery injury but won't stop it from occurring. Do not delay going to an ER.」
- 依據：「Efficacy is based on a 2018 study (Anfang et al) assessing the in vitro protective effects of various liquids in the cadaveric porcine esophagus and in vivo protective effects of honey and sucralfate ... in the esophagus of live piglets.」
- 電池加磁鐵：「Retrieve batteries, endoscopically if possible, from the stomach or beyond if: A magnet was also ingested, ...」

### Anfang 2019 與 Schmidt 2023（蜂蜜）

- Anfang RR 等，Laryngoscope 129(1):49-57，<https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.27312>，PMID 29889306。研究設計：「Cadaveric and live animal model.」結論：「early and frequent ingestion of honey in the household setting ... has the potential to reduce injury severity」。只有動物實驗。
- Schmidt YM 等 2023，Front Pediatr 11:1259780，<https://doi.org/10.3389/fped.2023.1259780>：「We found four publications ... Three of these presented experimental in vitro and in vivo results and one reported a clinical retrospective study of 8 patients.」「Follow up studies are required to further elucidate the effectiveness of the treatment with honey.」

蜂蜜的處理：證據只到動物實驗，國內廣東疾控寫「切勿……餵食」，兩邊衝突。所以不進標題、說人話和收益欄，只在備註裡把美國的條件和國內的說法並列寫，並寫明不滿 1 歲絕不能喂、別為找蜂蜜耽誤出門。

### 廣東省疾控中心（2025-03-18）

<https://cdcp.gd.gov.cn/jkjy/jkzt/zlkz/content/post_4686546.html>，curl 直取。

- 「廣東省產品傷害監測顯示，近五年與電池誤吞有關的傷害近百例，其中大多數與紐扣電池有關，受傷人群中92.9%為0～6歲的學齡前兒童。」
- 「紐扣電池誤吞傷害中，輕度傷害佔比60.7%，中度或重度傷害佔比39.3%，需住院處理的病例佔比高達25.0%。」
- 「如果懷疑兒童誤吞紐扣電池，應立即就醫，切勿自行給孩子催吐或餵食，專業醫療處理才是最有效的方法。」
- 巴克球：「成串的巴克球會堵塞腸管或相鄰腸管內的巴克球相互吸引擠壓腸管，從而造成腸梗阻或腸穿孔，嚴重時甚至會造成腹腔的重症感染，危及患兒生命。」「如果懷疑兒童誤吞巴克球，應立即送醫處理。」

### Kelaidi 2025（多顆磁鐵致腸瘻的文獻彙總）

<https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm14176235>，J Clin Med 14(17):6235，PMID 40943994。

- 「focusing on EEF after ingestion of ≥2 magnets in patients ≤18 years old」
- 「Sixty-nine studies encompassing 130 pediatric patients were included. Median age was 3.3 years」
- 「Surgical intervention was required in 95.5%」
- 「Postoperative complications occurred in 19%, including bowel obstruction, infection and one death.」

### ESPGHAN 2023 立場文件

Nugud AA 等，J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr 76(4):523-532，<https://doi.org/10.1097/MPG.0000000000003702>，PMID 36947000。「Magnet ingestion is a special category of foreign body ingestion associated with high levels of morbidity and mortality worldwide, particularly if it is associated with staggered ingestion of multiple magnets or with simultaneous ingestion of other metallic foreign bodies, especially button batteries.」只作來源，沒有取數字。

### 證據等級與收益量級

- B：數字來自中毒熱線、病例登記和病例彙總，指南建議沒有對照試驗。
- 收益「大」憑判斷：沒有「照做能少死多少」的降幅。依據是 2 小時就能燒出重傷、死亡病例多是沒人看見和被誤診拖過去，及時送醫拍片取出直接決定結局。
- 人群標籤 `嬰幼兒,孩子`：受傷的 92.9% 是 0 到 6 歲，覆蓋 3 歲以下和 3 到 18 歲兩檔。

## 第 46 條：有人喝醉了

### NHS Alcohol poisoning

<https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/alcohol-poisoning/>，帶代理 curl 取全文，頁面寫「Page last reviewed: 04 June 2026」。

- 症狀：「confusion」「slurring words or being unable to speak」「being unable to coordinate movement」「being sick」「peeing or pooing yourself」「pale or blue-tinged skin」「slow or irregular breathing」「having a seizure or fit」「loss of consciousness」
- 「Call 999 if: you think someone has alcohol poisoning; you or someone else has had a seizure or fit; someone has lost consciousness; someone has stopped breathing」
- 要做：「Do stay with them because there's a risk they could choke on their own sick or stop breathing」「sit them up if they're awake, or put them in the recovery position if they've passed out, and check they're breathing properly」「give them water to sip if they're conscious, able to swallow and not vomiting」「keep them warm with a jacket or blanket」
- 不要做：「do not let them drink more alcohol」「do not offer them food as they could choke」「do not give them coffee or drinks containing caffeine because this can dehydrate people with alcohol poisoning」「do not put them in a cold shower or bath because there's a risk they could get too cold, fall or lose consciousness in the water」「do not try to make them sick」

### 陝西省衛健委（2022-08-25，武漢協和醫院急診科孫鵬、彭操）

<https://sxwjw.shaanxi.gov.cn/ywgz/gbbj/202208/t20220825_2248211.html>

- 「窒息：醉酒者咽反射能力下降，嘔吐時易發生誤吸，特別是昏迷病人，輕者出現吸入性肺炎、肺部感染，重者可直接窒息死亡。」
- 「在急診工作中，我們常接收到一些已經死亡的醉酒者，從其氣管內吸出了大量嘔吐物，證明是由於誤吸致死的。」
- 昏睡期：「表現為面色蒼白、口唇青紫、皮膚溼冷、體溫下降、呼吸淺表、瞳孔擴大。」措施：「1.撥打“120”急救電話或緊急送醫救治。」「3.讓其保持側臥體位，不要催吐、餵食物，避免誤吸。」「4.給其披上毛毯或外套保暖，定時翻身，防止肢體長時間受壓。」「5.如發現呼吸心跳停止，立即進行心肺復甦。」
- 同一篇對共濟失調期寫的是「可以用刺激咽喉的辦法催吐」，和 NHS「do not try to make them sick」衝突。條目按 NHS 寫「別摳喉嚨催吐」，分歧寫進備註：叫不醒的不能催吐，中外一致。

### 北京市衛健委（2023-07-04，來源北京市垂楊柳醫院）

<https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jksh/202307/t20230706_3157010.html>

- 「如果患者昏迷，可將其擺放成側臥位（復甦位），以保持呼吸道通暢，便於嘔吐物排出。」
- 「對醉酒者千萬不要讓其單獨睡覺，以免患者發生窒息時無人救護。」
- 同一篇寫「對重度醉酒者，首先要催吐」，和 NHS、陝西那篇的昏睡期做法衝突，這句不引。

### 福建省藥監局（2022-02-28，轉載中國食品藥品網，作者王樹平）

<https://yjj.scjgj.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/kpzl/spyjts/ypaqjs/202202/t20220228_5846003.htm>

- 「醉酒患者因為咽反射能力降低，嘔吐時容易發生誤吸，而一旦發生誤吸，後果嚴重，輕者吸入性肺炎、肺部感染，重者可直接窒息致死。醉酒的患者一定要側臥」
- 「醉酒嚴重可抑制體溫的調節中樞，使體溫調節發生障礙，且低溫可導致心臟停搏。因此，酒精中毒救治中注意保暖。」

### Kanny 2015（美國疾控中心 MMWR）

MMWR 63(53):1238-1242，PMID 25577989，<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25577989/>（Europe PMC 沒給 DOI）。

- 「During 2010–2012, an annual average of 2,221 alcohol poisoning deaths (8.8 deaths per 1 million population) occurred among persons aged ≥15 years in the United States.」
- 「On average, six persons, mostly adult men, die from alcohol poisoning each day in the United States.」

### 同飲人的責任（第 3 檔受益人的法律面）

- 泰興市人民法院（2023-08-09），全國人民法庭信息網 <https://rmft.court.gov.cn/1150/jdal/3795.jhtml>：「酌定被告王五承擔 7% 的賠償責任，賠償原告 72720.9 元（ 1038870 元 ×7% ）；趙六承擔 3% 的賠償責任，賠償原告 31166.1 元（ 1038870 元 ×3% ）；李四、錢七不擔責。判決后王五不服提起上訴，泰州市中級人民法院作出二審維持判決。」「王五、趙六在與張三共同飲酒後，並未通知其家人或者將其安全送回家，存在相當過錯。」「共同飲酒人在飲酒過程中對其他同飲人負有提醒、勸阻、通知的義務，對醉酒者負有看扶、照顧、護送的義務」。條目裡的約 10.4 萬元（精確值 103887 元）是 72720.9 + 31166.1 自算。
- 最高人民法院指導性案例 227 號（2024-05-30 發佈），<https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/433761.html>，裁判要點 2：「經營者違反法律規定向未成年人售酒並供其飲用、同飲者或者共同從事危險活動者未盡到相應提醒和照顧義務，對該未成年人造成同一損害後果的，應當按照過錯程度、原因力大小等因素承擔相應的按份賠償責任。」這個案子的死者是未成年人，條目裡只引它寫明的「同飲者……按過錯分擔」這一層。

兩案都是事後打官司分責任，條目沒把它寫成「同飲就要賠」：泰興案裡中午一起喝過的李四、只在場勸阻的錢七都不擔責。

### 沒寫的

- 濃茶：只有西雙版納州政府轉載的人民網科普寫「勿用咖啡或濃茶解酒」，但同一篇主張重度醉酒者催吐，和 NHS 衝突，整篇不引。所以條目只寫 NHS 原文裡的「咖啡和含咖啡因的飲料」。
- 治安管理處罰法對醉酒者的保護性約束：沒核 2025 修訂版的條號，沒寫。

### 證據等級與收益量級

- B：官方科普和急診經驗，沒有對照數字。
- 收益「大」憑判斷：嗆死和呼吸停止發生在無人看護時，側躺加有人守著能及時發現、及時打 120。美國每年約 2221 人死於酒精中毒，但沒有「照做能少死多少」的降幅。
- 受益人：主線是家人，更常見的是第 3 檔（一起喝酒的朋友同事），陌生人一檔寫了「打 120、別上手搬」並指向第 2 條。
