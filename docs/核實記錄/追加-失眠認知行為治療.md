# 追加：第 3 節第 26 條，長期失眠先做失眠的認知行為治療，別一上來就長期吃安眠藥

2026-10-14 追加。四問：長期失眠很常見（規範寫患病率 10%～20%）；多數人以為失眠只能靠安眠藥或者「放鬆心態」，不知道有一線的非藥物治療；全書原有的睡眠條目只管睡多久、作息規律、午睡、補覺、咖啡因、屏幕（第 2 節第 13、37、38 條，第 3 節第 2、3、4、8、9、11 條），沒有一條管「已經失眠了怎麼辦」，第 34 節只在布洛芬那條提到含助眠成分的非處方藥；來源有國家衛健委診療規範、兩份美國指南和多篇薈萃分析。

放第 3 節：口徑按時間（少躺著睡不著的時間），掛進節首導覽「睡覺和熬夜」塊。

## 1. 國家衛健委《精神障礙診療規範（2020 年版）》
- 通知頁：<https://www.nhc.gov.cn/yzygj/c100068/202012/b4305ace9e14440792eb76d29602c88a.shtml>（curl 只拿到反爬殼頁，無頭 Chrome 不帶代理取到正文）。原文：「國衛辦醫函〔 2020 〕 945 號」「我委組織制定了《精神障礙診療 規範 （ 2020 年版 ）》。現印發給你們，請 參 照執行。」落款「國家衛生健康委辦公廳 2020年 11 月 23 日」，發佈時間 2020-12-07。
- 附件 PDF：<https://www.nhc.gov.cn/yzygj/c100068/202012/b4305ace9e14440792eb76d29602c88a/files/1733998716447_88723.pdf>（curl 帶瀏覽器 UA 可下，有文本層，pdftotext 逐字抽出）。目錄：「第十二章 睡眠障礙 …… 第二節 失眠障礙 ……325」。
- 患病率：「失眠障礙的患病率為 10%～20%」。
- 診斷要點：「根據 ICSD-3，失眠障礙的診斷要點包括：①存在入睡困難、睡 眠維持困難或早醒症狀；②日間疲勞、嗜睡，社會功能受損；③上述 症狀每週至少出現 3 次，持續至少 3 個月。如果病程小於 3 個月可稱 為短期失眠障礙。」
- 要先排除呼吸障礙：「在做出失眠 障礙的診斷前，須注意與焦慮、抑鬱等精神障礙的鑑別以及排除其他 常見睡眠障礙，如睡眠相關呼吸障礙、不寧腿綜合徵、睡眠-覺醒晝 夜節律障礙、睡眠不足綜合徵等。」
- 一線治療與五個部分：「失眠的認知行為治療 …… 是失眠障礙的一線治療方案。失眠認知行為治療主要包括睡眠 限制、刺激控制、認知治療、放鬆訓練治療和睡眠衛生 5 個部分。失 眠的認知行為治療一般以 6～8 周為一個週期，療效可延續 6～12 個 月。研究顯示，對於慢性失眠患者，失眠的認知行為治療與藥物療法 的短期療效相當，但長期來看失眠的認知行為治療療效優於藥物治 療。」
- 用藥：「連續 給藥一般不超過 4 周，如需繼續給藥，需每個月定期評估。」苯二氮䓬類「長期或高 劑量服用可能會產生戒斷現象、反跳性失眠、耐受、依賴等不良反應」。
- 中華醫學會神經病學分會 2017 版、中國睡眠研究會 2023 版指南原文沒取（期刊全文不開放），改用這份衛健委規範，是官方文件且能逐字核。

## 2. 美國醫師協會 2016 指南
- Qaseem A 等 (2016). Ann Intern Med 165(2):125-133. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M15-2175>，PMID 27136449，摘要經 Europe PMC 取得，卷期頁經同一接口核對。
- 原文：「ACP recommends that all adult patients receive cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) as the initial treatment for chronic insomnia disorder. (Grade: strong recommendation, moderate-quality evidence).」
- 原文：「ACP recommends that clinicians use a shared decision-making approach, including a discussion of the benefits, harms, and costs of short-term use of medications, to decide whether to add pharmacological therapy in adults with chronic insomnia disorder in whom cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) alone was unsuccessful.」

## 3. Trauer 2015 薈萃分析
- Trauer JM 等 (2015). Ann Intern Med 163(3):191-204. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M14-2841>，PMID 26054060，摘要經 Europe PMC 取得。
- 原文：「20 studies (1162 participants [64% female; mean age, 56 years]) were included.」
- 原文：「At the posttreatment time point, SOL improved by 19.03 (95% CI, 14.12 to 23.93) minutes, WASO improved by 26.00 (CI, 15.48 to 36.52) minutes, TST improved by 7.61 (CI, -0.51 to 15.74) minutes, and SE% improved by 9.91% (CI, 8.09% to 11.73%). Changes seemed to be sustained at later time points. No adverse outcomes were reported.」
- 限制：「Studies of insomnia comorbid with medical, sleep, or psychiatric disorders were excluded.」備註據此寫「排除了同時有別的病的失眠者」。

## 4. 長期效果（van der Zweerde / van Straten 2019）
- van der Zweerde T, Bisdounis L, Kyle SD, Lancee J, van Straten A (2019). Sleep Med Rev 48:101208. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smrv.2019.08.002>，PMID 31491656。任務裡寫的「van Straten 2018」實際查到的長期效果薈萃是這一篇（van Straten 為末位作者）。
- 原文：「we investigate long-term effects in 30 randomized controlled trials (RCTs) comparing CBT-I to non-active control groups.」「Hedges g for Insomnia severity index: 0.64 (3 m), 0.40 (6 m) and 0.25 (12 m)」「although effects decline over time, CBT-I produces clinically significant effects that last up to a year after therapy.」

## 5. 網上做（Zachariae 2016、Espie 2019）
- Zachariae R 等 (2016). Sleep Med Rev 30:1-10. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smrv.2015.10.004>，PMID 26615572。原文：「Eleven randomized controlled trials examining a total of 1460 participants were included.」「eCBT-I improved insomnia severity, sleep efficiency, subjective sleep quality, wake after sleep onset, sleep onset latency, total sleep time, and number of nocturnal awakenings at post-treatment, with effect sizes (Hedges's g) ranging from 0.21 to 1.09. The effects were comparable to those found for face-to-face CBT-I」「longer treatment duration and higher degree of personal clinical support were associated with larger effect sizes」。
- Espie CA 等 (2019). JAMA Psychiatry 76(1):21-30. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2018.2745>，PMID 30264137。原文：「This online, 2-arm, parallel-group randomized trial comparing dCBT for insomnia with sleep hygiene education (SHE) evaluated 1711 participants」「Use of dCBT was associated with a small improvement in functional health compared with SHE …… and psychological well-being …… and with a large improvement in sleep-related quality of life」。量表分值不好翻成日常話，條目只寫「小幅」「大幅」，不寫分值。

## 6. 安眠藥對老人（Glass 2005）
- Glass J 等 (2005). BMJ 331(7526):1169. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38623.768588.47>，PMID 16284208，PMC1285093。摘要經 Europe PMC，全文經 <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1285093/>（帶代理取得）。
- 摘要原文：「Randomised controlled trials of any pharmacological treatment for insomnia for at least five consecutive nights in people aged 60 or over」「24 studies (involving 2417 participants)」「total sleep time increased (mean 25.2 minutes, P < 0.001), and the number of night time awakenings decreased (0.63, P < 0.001)」「adverse cognitive events were 4.78 times more common (95% confidence interval 1.47 to 15.47, P < 0.01) …… reports of daytime fatigue were 3.82 times more common (1.88 to 7.80, P < 0.001)」
- 全文原文：「the number needed to harm for sedative hypnotics compared with placebo is 6 (4.7 to 7.1)」「The number needed to treat for improved sleep quality was 13 and the number needed to harm for any adverse event was 6. This ratio indicates that an adverse event is more than twice as likely as enhanced quality of sleep.」說人話「出副作用的是睡好了的兩倍多」按這句寫。
- 正文裡 4.78 寫成「odds ratio 4.78」，所以條目標 OR。
- 沒用的數：摘要寫精神運動類副作用「2.61 times more common (1.12 to 6.09, P > 0.05)」，全文寫「0.93 to 5.41, P = 0.07」，兩處對不上，不寫。

## 7. 刺激控制與睡眠限制的做法（AASM 2021）
- Edinger JD 等 (2021). J Clin Sleep Med 17(2):255-262. <https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.8986>，PMC7853203，全文經 PMC 網頁取得。
- 刺激控制：「Stimulus control instructions are: (a) go to bed only when sleepy; (b) get out of bed when unable to sleep; (c) use the bed/bedroom for sleep and sex only (no reading, watching TV, etc. in bed); (d) wake up the same time every morning; (e) refrain from daytime napping.」
- 睡眠限制：「A method designed to enhance sleep drive and consolidate sleep by limiting time in bed equal to the patient's sleep duration, typically estimated from daily diaries. Time in bed is initially limited to the average sleep duration, and subsequently increased or decreased based on sleep efficiency thresholds」
- 慎用人群：「this treatment may be contraindicated in certain populations such as those working in high risk occupations (eg, heavy machinery operators or drivers) or those predisposed to mania/hypomania poorly controlled seizure disorders or excessive daytime sleepiness」；白天更困：「increased daytime sleepiness and difficulties with concentration, but these effects typically dissipate as treatment progresses」；「if the treatment rationale is adequately explained and the patient is monitored by a clinician during treatment」。
- 單用睡眠衛生：「We suggest that clinicians not use sleep hygiene as a single-component therapy for the treatment of chronic insomnia disorder in adults. (CONDITIONAL)」

## 定級與收益量級
- 證據 A：核心結論來自隨機對照試驗的薈萃分析，有具體數字，國內外指南一致。未標爭議：沒找到反對「CBT-I 作一線」的指南或薈萃；短期療效與藥物相當這一點規範已寫明，條目照寫。
- 收益「中」：按時間口徑的閾值。入睡快 19.03 分鐘加夜裡醒著少 26.00 分鐘，每晚約 45 分鐘，一週約 5 小時，屬「每週小時級」。總睡眠時間只多 7.61 分鐘且不顯著，所以不按「每天小時級」定大。
- 成本標籤：錢=少（醫院幾次治療或網上課程，作者判斷，未查到國內統一收費標準）；時間=中（6～8 周療程加每天睡眠日記）；毅力=是（睡眠限制要連續幾周壓縮躺床時間、忍白天困）。不加人群標籤：慢性失眠沒有對應值。
- 「國內去睡眠門診、精神科、心理科」是作者經驗，C 級，沒有官方清單。
