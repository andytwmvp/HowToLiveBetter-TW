# 追加：第 27 節第 17 條，懷孕了不用把貓送走，貓砂換別人每天鏟，肉要做熟、碰土戴手套

2026-10-09。讀者 issue #100「對養寵物的建議」。用戶定：不新開節，只把有官方或原始文獻、能落到動作的幾條分散補進已有節，費用和保險不寫。本條是其中孕期那一處。

四問：① 養貓家庭裡懷孕很常見，會碰上；② 「懷孕要把貓送走」是讀者真信的說法，對照組孕婦裡也是貓被提得最多（見下文 Cook 2000 表 4）；③ 動作是新的，全書 grep「弓形蟲」「貓砂」「養貓」此前在正文條目裡零命中（第 34 節第 12 條備註只把貓砂當混藥的材料提到）；④ 有官方原文和原始研究。

## 1. 美國疾控中心 Risk Factors 頁
- URL：<https://www.cdc.gov/toxoplasmosis/risk-factors/index.html>（curl 直連和帶代理都 403，無頭 Chrome 帶代理取到，頁面標 Updated: February 14, 2025）
- 原文：「If you get infected with Toxoplasma for the first time while you are pregnant or just before your pregnancy, you can pass the infection to your baby.」「Most infected infants do not have symptoms at birth, but can develop serious symptoms later in life, such as blindness or mental disability」「Occasionally, infected newborns can have serious eye or brain damage at birth」
- 原文（給孕婦的做法）：「If possible, have someone else change cat litter. If no one else can, wear disposable gloves and wash your hands with soap and water afterwards.」「Keep cats indoors to prevent them from hunting other animals. This will reduce your cat's chances of becoming infected with Toxoplasma .」「Feed cats commercially prepared dry or canned food; do not feed cats raw or undercooked meats」「Do not adopt or handle stray cats, especially kittens. Do not get a new cat while you are pregnant or immunocompromised.」

## 2. 美國疾控中心 Preventing Toxoplasmosis 頁
- URL：<https://www.cdc.gov/toxoplasmosis/prevention/index.html>（同上，無頭 Chrome）
- 原文：「Change the cat litter box daily. The Toxoplasma parasite does not become infectious until 1 – 5 days after a cat shed it in its feces. By cleaning the litter box every day, you remove the feces before they become infectious.」「Rinse fruit and vegetables under running water.」「Wear gloves when gardening or when touching any soil or sand that may be contaminated with cat feces」「Do not drink unpasteurized goat's milk.」
- 同站 Causes 頁（<https://www.cdc.gov/toxoplasmosis/causes/index.html>，沒進來源欄）：「Kittens and cats can shed millions of parasites in their feces for as long as 3 weeks after infection. ## 5. Cook 2000 BMJ 歐洲六中心病例對照
- 題錄：Europe PMC，PMID 10894691，DOI 10.1136/bmj.321.7254.142，321(7254):142-147；全文 PMC27431（Europe PMC fullTextXML 返回 500，改從 pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov 帶代理取 HTML）。
- 原文：「A total of 252 infected women (cases) and 858 control women were enrolled」
- 表 1：「Any cat in home 42 206 124 697 1.0 (0.7 to 1.5) 0.99」「Cleaning litter tray 26 214 65 748 1.3 (0.8 to 2.1) 0.37」
- 表 2（多因素）：「Raw/undercooked lamb * 3.13 (1.4 to 7.2) 0.007」「Contact with soil * 1.81 (1.2 to 2.7) 0.005」。同表「Raw/undercooked beef * 1.73 (1.1 to 7.2) 0.01」的區間原文如此、看著像排印錯誤，沒用。
- 摘要：「Contact with cats was not a risk factor. Between 30% and 63% of infections in different centres were attributed to consumption of undercooked or cured meat products and 6% to 17% to soil contact.」
- 討論：「Contact with cats, kittens, cats' faeces, or cats who hunt for food was not a risk factor for infection.」「Contact with cats is not a risk factor for infection as excretion of oocysts is limited to only a few weeks」
- 侷限（備註據此）：「This route of transmission, however, was not considered in detail at interview」（指沒洗淨的生菜和生蔬菜）「In all centres a large proportion of infection (14% to 49%) remained unexplained」「All women and their interviewers were aware of the woman's infection status.」——事後回憶、雙方都知道是否感染，所以定 B。
- 表 4：對照組孕婦被問「怎麼防弓形蟲」時，提到貓的佔 49%，提到碰土的只有 6%。讀者真信「要防貓」由此佐證，沒寫進條目。

## 6. Dunn 1999 Lancet 母嬰傳播風險
- 題錄：Europe PMC，PMID 10359407，DOI 10.1016/s0140-6736(98)08220-8，353(9167):1829-1833。
- 摘要原文：「We analysed obstetric and paediatric data on 603 confirmed maternal toxoplasmosis infections. At least 564 women received antiparasitic drugs according to a standard protocol.」「The overall maternal-fetal transmission rate was 29% (95% CI 25-33), which masked a sharp increase in risk with duration of gestation from 6% at 13 weeks to 72% at 36 weeks. However, fetuses infected in early pregnancy were much more likely to show clinical signs of infection.」「women who seroconverted at 24-30 weeks of gestation carried the highest risk (10%) of having a congenitally infected child with early clinical signs」

## 定級
- 證據等級 B：貓和肉的風險數字只有一項病例對照研究，事後回憶、可能有偏差；做法來自 CDC、NHS、中國疾控中心，沒有試驗算過照做能少多少感染。沒標爭議：幾家官方機構方向一致，Cook 的「清貓砂盆 OR 1.3 不顯著」和「仍建議別人鏟、戴手套」不矛盾，後者是低成本的預防。
- 收益量級「中」憑判斷：死亡率口徑沒有相對降幅可套。後果是流產、死胎、孩子失明和智力殘疾，嚴重且不可逆；但孕期新感染本身少見，又沒有人群層面的效應量，所以不定「大」。成本幾乎為零、後果嚴重，所以不定「小」。
- 成本標籤：錢=0（交給家人鏟不花錢，手套幾塊錢），時間=少，毅力=些（肉要全熟、碰土戴手套是改習慣）。

## 沒收的
- 「備孕前帶貓查弓形蟲」「孕期減少和貓親密接觸」：只有中國疾控中心一家說，見上。
- 弓形蟲抗體篩查要不要做：只指路到產檢醫生，沒寫結論。
- 費用、寵物保險：用戶已定不寫。
