# 追加：第 13 節第 47 條（斷指保存）、第 48 條（恆牙撞掉）

2026-10-14。主任務給的兩個候選，都追加在第 13 節末尾，沒有插在中間。節首導覽掛進「外傷和燙傷」一塊，短名「手指被切斷」「恆牙整顆撞掉」。

查重：全書 grep「斷指、斷肢、離斷、再植、撞掉、牙外傷、牙槽」，正文沒有同主題條目。第 1 節第 42 條（角磨機）的核實記錄提到日本斷指病例，只用來說明傷得多重，沒有保存斷指的動作。第 13 節第 12 條（大出血）管止血，第 47 條只指過去，不重複。

四問：① 切菜、門縫夾手、電動工具切斷手指，孩子摔跤撞掉門牙，都常見；② 常見的錯誤做法是把斷指直接埋進冰裡、泡進水裡，牙用紙巾包著幹放或者扔掉，讀者多半不知道；③ 動作是新的；④ 有 NLM、NHS、IADT 原文。

## 第 47 條 斷指

### 1. MedlinePlus, Traumatic amputation（Review Date 8/27/2024）
- URL：<https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000006.htm>（2026-10-14 帶代理 curl 取到）
- 「Control bleeding by applying direct pressure to the wound. Raise the injured area.」
- 「If the person has life-threatening bleeding, a tight bandage or tourniquet will be easier to use than direct pressure on the wound.」
- 「Save any severed body parts and make sure they stay with the person. If possible, remove any dirty material that can contaminate the wound, then gently rinse the body part if the cut end is dirty.」
- 「Wrap the severed part in a clean, damp cloth, place it in a sealed plastic bag and place the bag in an ice water bath.」
- 「Do not put the body part directly in water or ice without using a plastic bag.」
- 「Do not put the severed part directly on ice. Do not use dry ice as this will cause frostbite and injury to the part.」
- 「If cold water is not available, keep the part away from heat as much as possible. ... Cooling the severed part allows a longer window of time for later reattachment. Without cooling, the severed part is only good for reattachment for about 4 to 6 hours.」
- 「Do not forget that saving the person's life is more important than saving a body part.」「Do not attempt to push any part or bone back into place.」「Do not decide that a body part is too small to save.」
- 「4 到 6 小時」只按這一句寫，原文說的是「不冷著」的情形。冷著能撐多久原文沒給數字，條目沒寫。

### 2. AAOS OrthoInfo, Fingertip Injuries and Amputations
- URL：<https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/diseases--conditions/fingertip-injuries-and-amputations>
- 「Gently clean the amputated part with water or, if possible, with sterile saline solution. Cover it in a moistened gauze wrap. Place it in a sealed watertight bag ... Place the bag on ice in a sealed container or another watertight bag. Do not allow the amputated part to be in direct contact with ice. You could damage it further. Go immediately to the emergency room, preferably at a hospital that has hand specialists immediately available.」
- 美國手外科學會（ASSH）頁面用 curl 只拿到 3 KB 殼頁，沒用。

### 3. 再植存活率與缺血時間（Europe PMC 摘要）
- Treger D 等 (2026) HAND，DOI 10.1177/15589447241279445：「Of the 31 included studies, 26 studies reported that 1976 digits were successfully replanted, while 27 studies reported that 300 replants failed (86.8% success rate). Among 1087 patients in these studies, 82.9% who underwent replantation returned to work.」
- Stone N 等 (2021) Microsurgery，DOI 10.1002/micr.30738：「12 retrospective cohort studies met inclusion criteria ... The overall replantation survival rate was 85.3%.」
- Yu F 等 (2026) Front Surg，DOI 10.3389/fsurg.2026.1795247：「A total of 12 studies involving 3,645 patients were included. ... preoperative ischemic time ≥8 h [OR = 2.25, 95% CI (1.43, 3.54)] ... may be associated with necrosis after digital replantation.」條目按「幾率約 2.25 倍」寫，保留 OR 原值。
- 反方或不一致：Ma Z 等 (2016) J Hand Surg Eur，DOI 10.1177/1753193415594572 摘要說「Gender and ischemia time had no significant influence on the survival rate」，同時說保存方法有顯著影響，但摘要沒寫哪種方法好，所以沒進條目。缺血時間的影響各研究說法不一，條目只寫了 8 小時這一個門檻。
- Huang RS 等 (2023) 兒童斷指再植綜述：存活率 76%，撕脫、壓傷比切割傷存活幾率低。條目備註「壓爛的、撕扯下來的比切得整齊的難接」據此，沒寫數字。

### 證據等級與收益
- B：保存方法是指南和教科書共識，沒有比較保存方法的對照試驗；存活率來自回顧性研究的合併。
- 收益「中」憑判斷：口徑歸死亡率（含健康終點），但終點是保住手指，不是活命，相對降幅沒法按閾值套。

## 第 48 條 恆牙撞掉

### 1. NHS, Knocked-out tooth（Page last reviewed: 06 February 2025）
- URL：<https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/knocked-out-tooth/>
- 「Hold the tooth by the white bit that sticks out of the gum (the crown) – do not touch the root.」
- 「If the tooth is dirty, rinse it gently in milk, saline or saliva.」
- 「Try to put it back into the hole in the gum.」「Bite down gently on a clean cloth to hold the tooth in place if it goes back in easily.」「Go to a dentist as soon as possible.」
- 「Do not put a baby tooth back in as it could damage the tooth growing underneath.」
- 「If you cannot put the tooth back in, you can put it in either milk or saliva.」
- 「Ask for an emergency dentist appointment if: you have knocked out a tooth, even if you cannot find it」
- 「If you cannot find the tooth or the dentist is not able to save it, the tooth may be replaced with a denture (false tooth) while the socket heals. Once it's healed, there may be other options like a bridge or dental implant.」

### 2. IADT「Save your tooth」海報（英文版圖片）
- 頁面：<https://iadt-dentaltrauma.org/guidelines-and-resources/save-a-tooth-poster/>，英文圖 <https://iadt-dentaltrauma.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Save-Tooth-ENGLISH528w.jpg>（用 Read 看圖核對）
- 步驟原文：「Find the tooth」「Hold it by the crown」「(plug the sink) Rinse in cold tap water」「FOLLOW ONE OF THESE ALTERNATIVES: a Put the tooth back in its place / b Place the tooth in a cup of milk or saline / c When milk is not available, place the tooth in the mouth between the cheeks and gums」「Seek immediate specialized dental treatment, within a two hour time period」
- 海報寫的「two hour」和研究裡「幹放 1 小時」不是同一個口徑（前者是就診時限，後者是牙在體外幹著的時間），條目兩個都沒寫成就診時限，只寫「馬上去看牙」。
- 中文版海報 PDF 下載到了（2.5 MB），pdftotext 抽不出字，沒用。

### 3. IADT 2020 指南（未取得正文）
- Fouad AF 等 (2020). International Association of Dental Traumatology guidelines for the management of traumatic dental injuries: 2. Avulsion of permanent teeth. Dental Traumatology（卷期頁碼沒核）. DOI 10.1111/edt.12573，PMID 32460393。
- Semantic Scholar 標為開放獲取（CC BY-NC-ND），但 Wiley 全文頁和 pdfdirect 都被 Cloudflare 擋住，curl 和無頭 Chrome（帶代理、90 秒）都只拿到驗證頁。摘要只有總述，沒有具體動作。所以條目來源欄沒列它，動作按 NHS 和 IADT 海報核對。
- 中華口腔醫學會的牙外傷指南沒有去找（本機查國內學會指南沒有現成通道），條目沒引。

### 4. 骨性粘連風險（Europe PMC 摘要）
- Gul A 等 (2024) Dent Traumatol，DOI 10.1111/edt.12898：「Data from 116 patients with 145 replanted avulsed permanent teeth were selected from a comprehensive dental trauma database in Copenhagen University Hospital.」「follow-up ranged from 7 months to 23 years」「The overall risk of ankylosis was 17.2% [95% CI: 4.61; 29.79] for immediately replanted teeth, 55.3% [95% CI: 42.54; 68.00] for teeth stored in physiologic media before replantation, and 85.7% [95% CI: 75.70; 95.73] for teeth stored dry more than 1 h.」生理介質指口水和生理鹽水（「physiologic storage media (saliva and saline)」）。
- Lauridsen E 等 (2020) Dent Traumatol，DOI 10.1111/edt.12520，單位 University Hospital, Copenhagen：「The material originates from a previously reported clinical study of 400 replanted teeth.」「The estimated risks of ankylosis after 900 days for teeth with immature root development were: dry time < 20 minutes: 33.9% ... dry time > 60 minutes: 60.0% ... The risks for teeth with mature root development were: dry time < 20 minutes: 59.4% ... dry time > 60 minutes: 86.4%」「However, some teeth may heal without ankylosis even after 60 minutes of dry time. ... Replantation should therefore always be considered for avulsed teeth.」摘要裡的 HR 1.22 沒寫單位（每多少分鐘），條目沒用。
- Andreasen JO 等 (1995) 第 1 篇，DOI 10.1111/j.1600-9657.1995.tb00461.x，單位 Rigshospitalet：「During the observation period, 119 teeth (30%) were extracted.」
- Andreasen JO 等 (1995) 第 4 篇，DOI 10.1111/j.1600-9657.1995.tb00464.x：「Storage in tap water for more than 20 minutes usually led to root resorption.」「Based on these findings, immediate replantation is recommended irrespective of stage of root development.」

### 證據等級與收益
- B：一家醫院的隨訪隊列，沒有分組對照。
- 收益「中」憑判斷：終點是牙根粘連，不是直接掉牙，更不是死亡；粘連風險從 85.7% 降到 17.2% 的相對降幅很大，但這是替代性的健康終點，不按死亡率閾值機械套「大」。
- 人群標籤沒加：撞掉牙多見於孩子，但大人摔跤、運動撞到嘴一樣適用，按規則不加。
