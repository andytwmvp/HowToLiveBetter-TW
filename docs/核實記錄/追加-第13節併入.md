# 第 13 節併入：熱性驚厥、長途旅行防血栓（2026-10-14）

## 第 16 條（有人抽搐倒地）併入孩子發燒時抽搐

標題末尾加了「；孩子發燒時抽搐也一樣」，原字未刪。成本標籤不加人群（成人同樣適用）。

### NHS. Febrile seizures

<https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/febrile-seizures/>（2026-10-14 帶代理取得，頁面寫 Page last reviewed: 21 June 2023）

逐字摘錄：

- 「A febrile seizure can seem frightening, but it's unlikely to harm your child or cause long-term effects.」
- 「Febrile seizures usually last for 2 to 3 minutes. It's rare for them to last more than 10 minutes.」
- 「They usually affect children between 6 months and 6 years old.」
- Call 999 or go to A&E if your child：「has a seizure for the first time」「has a seizure that lasts more than 5 minutes」「is having difficulty breathing」「has stiffness and twitching on only 1 side of their body」「is more sleepy than usual for more than 1 hour after the seizure stops」「has more than 1 seizure within 24 hours」
- 「Do not take your child to A&E if they are still having a seizure or are unconscious, call 999 instead.」
- Do：「check the time the seizure starts and ends」「protect their head with your hands or something soft」「remove any dangerous objects that are nearby」「after the seizure ends, put your child in the recovery position and make sure there is nothing in their mouth or throat that might affect their breathing」
- Don't：「do not move your child or try to hold them in place」「do not put anything in your child's mouth」「do not try to lower your child's temperature by undressing them or sponging them with cold water」
- 「But your child will need to be checked in hospital after having a febrile seizure for the first time.」

### AAP 2011

Subcommittee on Febrile Seizures, American Academy of Pediatrics (2011). Neurodiagnostic evaluation of the child with a simple febrile seizure. Pediatrics 127(2):389-394. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2010-3318>，PMID 21285335。摘要取自 Europe PMC：

- 「To formulate evidence-based recommendations … about the diagnosis and evaluation of a simple febrile seizure in infants and young children 6 through 60 months of age」
- 「Clinicians evaluating infants or young children after a simple febrile seizure should direct their attention toward identifying the cause of the child's fever.」
- 「In general, a simple febrile seizure does not usually require further evaluation, specifically electroencephalography, blood studies, or neuroimaging.」

這份指南講的是醫生怎麼檢查，不講現場急救；只用來說明「單純熱性驚厥一般不用做腦電圖、抽血、腦部影像」。

## 第 11 條（一條腿突然腫起來）併入長途旅行

標題沒加字：原標題已約 45 字，再加「坐 4 小時以上」一段會過長，只改了說人話、收益、來源、備註。

### WHO WRIGHT 項目（2007 年新聞稿，來源欄原有）

<https://www.who.int/news/item/29-06-2007-study-results-released-on-travel-and-blood-clots>

- 「the risk of developing venous thromboembolism (VTE) approximately doubles after travel lasting four hours or more」「about 1 in 6000」
- 「plane, train, bus or automobile passengers are at higher risk of VTE when they remain seated and immobile on journeys of more than four hours」
- 「This study did not investigate effective preventive measures against DVT and VTE. However, experts recognize that blood circulation can be promoted by exercising the calf muscles with up-and-down movements of the feet at the ankle joints.」
- 「People should also avoid wearing tight clothing during travel」
- 「Individuals with questions regarding prevention of VTE should consult their physicians before travelling.」

WRIGHT 第一階段報告全文沒有另找；新聞稿已寫明研究不檢驗預防辦法，所以「起來走動」的依據改用 NHS。

### NHS. DVT (deep vein thrombosis)

<https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt/>（Page last reviewed: 30 April 2026）

- 「If you're travelling for 4 hours or more by plane, train or car, there are things you can do during the journey to lower your chances of getting DVT.」後列「wearing loose clothing」「drinking plenty of water」「avoiding alcohol」「walking around when possible」
- 「do not sit still for long periods of time – get up and move around every hour or so」

### Cochrane 2021

Clarke MJ, Broderick C, Hopewell S, Juszczak E, Eisinga A (2021). Compression stockings for preventing deep vein thrombosis in airline passengers. Cochrane Database Syst Rev 4:CD004002. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004002.pub4>，PMID 33878207。摘要取自 Europe PMC：

- 「Twelve randomised trials (n = 2918) were included in this review」「All flights had a duration of more than five hours.」
- 「Eight trials included people judged to be at low or medium risk of developing DVT (n = 1598) and two included high-risk participants (n = 1273).」
- 「Fifty of 2637 participants with follow-up data available in the trials of wearing compression stockings on both legs had a symptomless DVT; three wore stockings, 47 did not (odds ratio (OR) 0.10, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.04 to 0.25, P < 0.001; high-certainty evidence).」
- 「No deaths, pulmonary emboli or symptomatic DVTs were reported.」
- 「We cannot assess the effect of wearing stockings on death, pulmonary embolism or symptomatic DVT because no such events occurred in these trials.」

終點是沒症狀、靠檢查查出來的血栓，屬於替代終點，正文如實寫了「能不能防住死亡、肺栓塞還算不出來」，也因此只寫「有高危因素的問醫生要不要穿」，不寫人人都穿。證據等級維持 B，收益量級維持原來的「大」（由確診肺栓塞的死亡率定，與壓力襪無關）。
