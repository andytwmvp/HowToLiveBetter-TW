# 待核清單處理：第 2、3、4 節（2026-10-14）

對應 `全書審校待核清單-2026-10-14.md` 的第 2、3 節。清單裡沒有第 4 節的項，「重審時新報的」裡也沒有這三節的項。

## 第 2 節

### 第 1 條備註「換一種算法差別就不顯著」
- 查：Nåsell 2010 摘要，Europe PMC PMID 20516308，<https://doi.org/10.2106/JBJS.I.00627>
- 原文：「The proportion of patients with at least one postoperative complication was significantly larger in the control group … (38% and 20%, respectively; p = 0.048)」「A secondary analysis showed that the odds of having a complication were 2.51 times (95% confidence interval, 0.96 to 6.9 times) higher … but this difference was not significant.」
- 處理：核實是對的。備註改成「作者換成比值比又算了一遍，差別就不顯著了（OR 2.51，95% CI 0.96–6.9）」。

### 第 3 條備註：伐尼克蘭副作用、尼古丁替代別驟停
- 查：Cochrane CD006103.pub8 全文（PMC10169257）；DailyMed NicoDerm CQ 說明書 <https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=93b2d1b9-83c1-40b5-b6af-90c38c8d6cef>
- 原文：「people randomised to receive varenicline were more likely to report experiencing nausea (RR 2.61, 95% CI 2.44 to 2.80 …), insomnia (RR 1.37, 95% CI 1.27 to 1.47 …), abnormal dreams (RR 1.82 …」「The most common unwanted effect of varenicline is nausea, but this is mostly at mild or moderate levels and usually clears over time.」說明書：「use according to the following 10-week schedule: STEP 1 STEP 2 STEP 3 Use one 21 mg patch/day Use one 14 mg patch/day Use one 7 mg patch/day Weeks 1-6 Weeks 7-8 Weeks 9-10」
- 處理：副作用核實是對的，數字補進收益欄。「有精神科病史的要跟醫生說明」沒有找到對應原文（綜述裡有精神科病史人群的試驗，但沒說要告知醫生），標成作者的建議。「別自己突然停，按醫生說的減量」改成「貼片按說明書或醫生說的一步步減量再停」，DailyMed 說明書補進來源欄，分步方案寫進收益欄。

### 第 6 條備註：WHO 不推薦、英國納入戒菸服務、「上頭電子煙」來源
- 查：WHO 2023-12-14 新聞 <https://www.who.int/news/item/14-12-2023-urgent-action-needed-to-protect-children-and-prevent-the-uptake-of-e-cigarettes>；英國衛生部 2023-04-11 新聞稿 <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/smokers-urged-to-swap-cigarettes-for-vapes-in-world-first-scheme>；最高檢轉載法治日報 <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>
- 原文：WHO「E-cigarettes as consumer products are not shown to be effective for quitting tobacco use at the population level.」「it is not recommended that governments permit sale of e-cigarettes as consumer products in pursuit of a cessation objective.」「should … regulate the products as medicines」「Whilst long-term health effects are not fully understood」「E-cigarettes have been allowed on the open market and aggressively marketed to young people.」英國「almost 1 in 5 of all smokers in England will be provided with a vape starter kit alongside behavioural support」「One million smokers will be encouraged to swap cigarettes for vapes」。最高檢「這種“上頭”電子煙內含有被國家整類列管的合成大麻素成分」。
- 處理：原文說的和備註不完全一樣。WHO 說的是「當普通商品賣，沒證據顯示能在人群裡幫人戒菸，不建議政府為戒菸放開銷售，要用就當藥管」，不是籠統的「不推薦當戒菸手段」；「英國放進了戒菸服務」改成英格蘭 2023 年「以電子煙換香菸」計劃。三處來源補進來源欄。

### 第 8 條：口腔癌、食管癌劑量反應、含菸草檳榔更危險
- 查：IARC 第 100E 卷 PDF（monographs.iarc.who.int）本機只拿到 HTML 殼頁，NCBI Bookshelf 是驗證碼頁，都沒取到。改用 Europe PMC 三篇薈萃：Guha 2014 <https://doi.org/10.1002/ijc.28643>，Akhtar 2013 <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10552-012-0113-9>，Shah 2025 <https://doi.org/10.1158/1055-9965.EPI-24-1502>
- 原文：Guha「The meta-relative risk (mRR) for oral/oropharyngeal cancer in the Indian subcontinent was 2.56 (95%CI, 2.00-3.28; 15 studies) for BQ-T and 7.74 (95%CI, 5.38-11.13; 31 studies) for BQ+T; in Taiwan, China, the mRR for BQ-T was 10.98 (95%CI, 4.86-24.84; 13 studies).」「the risk of oral/oropharyngeal cancer increased with increasing daily amount and duration (years) of chewing BQ in India and Taiwan, China.」Akhtar「adjusted main-effect summary OR(RE) = 3.05; 95 % CI 2.41, 3.87」「adjusted additive interaction-effect summary OR(RE) = 6.79; 95 % CI 4.71, 9.79」。Shah「reduced head and neck cancer risk following cessation of betel quid use with (RR = 0.66; 95% CI, 0.54-0.81) or without tobacco (RR = 0.73; 95% CI, 0.56-0.95)」「Long-term cessation (≥20 years) conferred substantial benefits (RR = 0.37; 95% CI, 0.22-0.61」
- 處理：劑量反應、食管癌、戒後下降核實是對的，數字進收益欄、三篇進來源欄。「含菸草的檳榔更危險」按原文收窄：只在印度次大陸的數據里加菸草的更高，臺灣不加菸草的風險也很高（10.98），備註改成這樣。

### 第 10 條：標題「缺牙及時補」只有關聯證據；「圓頭電動牙刷證據最確定」
- 查：Yaacob 2014（CD002281.pub3）與 Deacon 2010（CD004971.pub2）摘要，Europe PMC
- 原文：Yaacob「rotation oscillation (27 trials) … The greatest body of evidence was for rotation oscillation brushes which demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in plaque and gingivitis at both time points.」Deacon「Due to the dearth of trials assessing the same mode of action, no definitive conclusions can be stated regarding the superiority of one mode of powered toothbrush over any other.」
- 處理：「最確定」按原文改成「試驗最多（27 項），短期和長期都看到牙菌斑和牙齦炎明顯減少」。標題「缺牙及時補」：沒有研究測過補牙能不能降死亡，備註補一句「建議補是因為缺牙影響吃東西，這是作者的判斷」。標題沒改，列進回報。

### 第 11 條：Ekelund 2019 備註斷言
- 查：Ekelund 2019 全文（PMC6699591）
- 原文：「individual level data from eight studies (n=36 383; mean age 62.6 years; 72.8% women), with median follow-up of 5.8 years」「We attempted to minimise bias from reverse causation by excluding all deaths within the first two years in sensitivity analyses. The hazard ratios were materially unchanged … However, bias from reverse causation might persist.」
- 處理：「跟蹤時間短，參與者偏老」核實是對的，數字補進收益欄。「真實好處沒有 0.52 倍那麼大」原文沒這麼說（去掉頭兩年死亡後結果基本不變，只說偏差可能還在），改成「可能說得偏大，這是本書的推斷」，並在收益欄寫明論文作者的敏感性分析。

### 第 30 條：潮汕、太行山食管癌與趁燙喝
- 查：Europe PMC 檢索 Chaoshan/Shantou/Taihang 加 hot/temperature 加 esophageal，只找到病例報告和影像研究的引言順帶一句，沒有研究這兩個地區燙食與食管癌關係的原始文獻。改用國內全國性隊列 Yu 2018（中國慢性病前瞻性研究）<https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2000>
- 原文：「those who drank burning-hot tea and 15 g or more of alcohol daily had the greatest risk for esophageal cancer (hazard ratio [HR], 5.00 [95% CI, 3.64 to 6.88]). Likewise, the HR for current smokers who drank burning-hot tea daily was 2.03 (CI, 1.55 to 2.67).」「Drinking tea at high temperatures is associated with an increased risk for esophageal cancer when combined with excessive alcohol or tobacco use.」參照組是「participants who drank tea less than weekly and consumed fewer than 15 g of alcohol daily」。
- 處理：潮汕、太行山那句核不到，刪掉。換成 Yu 2018 的國內數據，寫進備註，來源補進來源欄。

### 第 31 條：備註斷言
- 查：Lindqvist 2016 摘要，PMID 26992108
- 原文：「Women with active sunlight exposure habits experience a lower mortality rate than women who avoid sun exposure; however, they are at an increased risk of skin cancer.」
- 處理：「曬太陽會增加皮膚癌風險」核實是對的，改成「論文作者自己也寫明，常曬太陽的女性得皮膚癌的風險更高」。「迴避曬太陽的人可能本來就體弱、不愛出門」摘要裡沒有，標成作者的推測。

### 第 33 條：胃食管反流、痔瘡、腸易激吃辣加重
- 查：沒有找到能逐字核對的官方或薈萃原文。
- 處理：常識性提醒，「會加重」改「可能加重」，標成作者經驗。

### 第 35 條：「每天三四個」門檻；美國膳食指南取消膽固醇上限
- 查：2015–2020 美國膳食指南 PDF <https://www.dietaryguidelines.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/2015-2020_Dietary_Guidelines.pdf>（pdftotext 逐字取）；Drouin-Chartier 2020 BMJ 摘要 <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.m513>
- 原文：指南「The Key Recommendation from the 2010 Dietary Guidelines to limit consumption of dietary cholesterol to 300 mg per day is not included in the 2015 edition, but this change does not suggest that dietary cholesterol is no longer important to consider when building healthy eating patterns. As recommended by the IOM, individuals should eat as little dietary cholesterol as possible while consuming a healthy eating pattern.」Drouin-Chartier「consumption of at least one egg per day was not associated with incident cardiovascular disease risk … (hazard ratio … 0.93, 95% confidence interval 0.82 to 1.05)」「an increase of one egg per day was not associated with cardiovascular disease risk (pooled relative risk 0.98, 95% confidence interval 0.93 to 1.03」「moderate egg consumption (up to one egg per day) is not associated with cardiovascular disease risk overall」
- 處理：膳食指南那句原文說的不一樣：取消了上限，但同時說要儘量少吃，備註按原文補全。「一天一個不必擔心」按 Drouin-Chartier 收窄成「和心血管病沒看出關聯」（它看的是心血管病，不是總死亡），這篇補進收益欄和來源欄。「也有研究沒發現雞蛋和死亡率有關」改成「和心血管病有關」。說人話刪掉「只是別每天吃三四個」，換成收益欄裡有的那句。「三四個」這個門檻沒有研究依據，備註寫明是作者的判斷。標題沒改，列進回報。

### 第 36 條：日本浴缸猝死
- 查：日本消費者廳提醒頁 <https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_safety/caution/caution_084/>；Ukai 2020 摘要
- 原文：「「浴槽內での及び浴槽への転落による溺死及び溺水」による死亡者數は、令和6年に7,776人となっています。」「暖かい室內と寒い脫衣所や浴室との溫度差などによる急激な血圧の変動や、熱いお湯をはった浴槽內に長くつかることによる體溫上昇での意識障害などが挙げられています。」Ukai「A total of 30 076 participants aged 40-59 years」
- 處理：原文是「浴缸裡溺死」，不是「猝死」，統計不分年齡。「每年有大量老人在浴缸裡猝死」改成「2024 年在浴缸裡溺死的有 7776 人」加消費者廳說的原因。順帶把 Ukai 研究對象 40 到 59 歲補進收益欄。

### 第 39 條：「唯一被證明能搬動生物鐘」
- 查：Czeisler 1990 摘要 PMID 2325721；docs/生物鐘和夜班.md 第八節
- 原文：摘要只說「maladaptation … can be treated effectively with scheduled exposure to bright light at night and darkness during the day」，沒有「唯一」。長文也沒寫「唯一」。
- 處理：「唯一」核不到，改成「後者在實驗裡真把生物鐘搬了過去」。CLAUDE.md 目錄簡介裡也有「唯一被證明真能搬動鐘的辦法」，我不許改，交主任務。

### 第 44 條：瑞典數據口徑
- 查：Mubanga 2017 全文（PMC5693989），Mubanga 2019 摘要
- 原文：「In single- and multiple-person households, dog ownership (13.1%) was associated with lower risk of death, HR 0.67 (95% CI, 0.65-0.69) and 0.89 (0.87-0.91)」；全文「Dog ownership was inversely associated with … all-cause mortality (adjusted HR 0.80, 95% CI, 0.79–0.82」；校正項「sex, marital status, presence of children in the home, population density, area of residence, region of birth, income and latitude」。2019 年心梗後「HR 0.67 (95% CI, 0.61 to 0.75) for those who lived alone, and HR of 0.85 (95% CI, 0.80 to 0.90) for those living with a partner or a child」，中風後 0.73、0.88。
- 處理：核實是對的，收益欄原有數字都對得上。補一句全體合併後的 HR 0.80。

## 第 3 節

### 第 2 條備註「配合早上見光才有效果」
- 查：Phillips 2017 全文（PMC5468315）
- 原文：「A mathematical model … was used to demonstrate that Irregular vs. Regular group differences in circadian timing were likely primarily due to their different patterns of light exposure.」「our results suggest this could be treated in undergraduates through light interventions used to advance circadian rhythms」「light-based interventions may be therapeutically effective」
- 處理：原文沒說「早上」，也沒做干預試驗，「才有效果」說過頭。改成「研究者用模型推算……建議可以試試用光來調，但沒有做試驗驗證。白天多出門見光是從他們的推算推出來的」。

### 第 8 條標題「墨水屏」沒測過
- 查：Chang 2015 全文 <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4313820/>
- 原文：「comparing the effects of reading before bedtime using a light-emitting eReader (LE-eBook) with reading a printed book by reflected light」「reading an LE-eBook (iPad …) in otherwise very dim room light for ∼4 h before bedtime for five consecutive evenings」「the LE-eBook was set to maximum brightness throughout the 4-h reading session」
- 處理：標題加字成「要看就看紙書或不開屏幕燈的墨水屏」。備註寫明墨水屏沒測，不開燈的墨水屏歸到紙書這邊是從「紙書靠反射光」推出來的，開燈的沒有數據。收益欄補上 iPad、最高亮度、約 4 小時、連 5 晚這些實驗條件。

### 第 18 條標題「曬太陽」，證據是燈箱光照治療
- 查：Golden 2005 是光照治療薈萃，沒有曬太陽的研究。
- 處理：備註補「那些研究用的是專門的光照燈，不是曬太陽；換成白天出門曬太陽是從光照治療推出來的」。標題沒改，列進回報。

### 第 23 條標題「砸東西」收益欄沒有
- 查：Kjærvik 2024 摘要，PMID 38518585
- 原文：「others focus on increasing arousal (e.g., hitting a bag, jogging, cycling)」「These findings do not support the ideas that venting anger or going for a run are effective anger management activities.」
- 處理：原文舉例只有打沙袋、慢跑、騎車。備註補「砸東西這項研究沒單獨測過，它和打沙袋一樣讓身體更興奮，歸到同一類是作者的推論」。標題沒改，列進回報。
