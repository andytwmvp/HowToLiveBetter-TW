# 追加：第 27 節第 18 條，懷孕期間趕上流感季就打一針流感滅活疫苗

2026-10-14 追加。四問：孕婦是流感重症的高危人群，讀者普遍碰得上；國內有的疫苗說明書過去把孕婦列為禁忌，很多人以為孕期不能打；全書原有的流感疫苗條目只有第 1 節第 20 條（心血管病人和老年人），沒寫孕婦和 6 月齡以下嬰兒；來源是隨機試驗、彙總分析、WHO 立場文件和中國疾控中心年度指南原文。

全書查重：`grep -n 流感 book/*.md`，孕婦接種在全書沒有出現過。第 20 節新生兒那幾條只寫國家免疫規劃疫苗，流感疫苗不在免疫規劃裡，不重複。

## 1. WHO 立場文件（2022 年 5 月）
- 題錄：Vaccines against influenza: WHO position paper – May 2022. Weekly Epidemiological Record, No 19, 2022, 97, 185–208。PDF 經 iris.who.int 的 DSpace 接口取得（`/server/api/core/bitstreams/search/byItemHandle?handle=10665/354264&filename=WER9719-eng-fre.pdf` 給出 content 鏈接），pdftotext 逐字抽出。iris 的 bitstream/handle 舊鏈接只返回前端殼頁。
- 孕婦住院風險：「A systematic review14 in 2017 and a meta-analysis15 in 2019 showed that influenza during pregnancy resulted in a 7 times higher risk of hospital admission (OR 6.8, 95% CI 6–7.7) but a lower risk of admission to ICU (OR 0.6, 95% CI 0.5–0.7); it was not significantly associated with an increased risk of death (OR 1, 95% CI 0.8–1.3) compared with non-pregnant individuals.」
  - 注意：同一句說孕婦得流感**死亡風險並不顯著更高**。所以條目只寫住院，不寫「孕婦得流感更容易死」。中國疾控指南寫的「死亡（OR=3.62）」和 WHO 這裡的死胎 RR 3.62 數字相同、出處不同，沒核到原文，不採用。
- 推薦：「Influenza vaccines are safe and effective in preventing infection in pregnant individuals, complications during the pregnancy and influenza infection in the baby in its first few months of life. Any of the currently available inactivated or recombinant seasonal influenza vaccines authorized as age-appropriate may be used, since there are demonstrated benefits over no vaccination.」
- 任何階段：「Vaccination may be administered at any stage of pregnancy, preferably prior to the start of the influenza season. The risk of influenza infection to the mother, and thus to the fetus, is higher than that associated with the inactivated influenza vaccine, which has a wellestablished safety record.」
- 減毒活疫苗：「Because LAIV is a live-virus vaccine and data on its administration to pregnant women and the associated maternal and fetal risks are limited, LAIV is also not recommended during pregnancy.」
- 安全史：「IIVs have been administered to pregnant women for the past 50 years. Clinical trials and observational studies in pregnant women, including studies that have looked at fetal death, spontaneous abortion and congenital malformations, have found no evidence of any IIVassociated adverse effects in either the women or their newborn babies.」

## 2. Zaman 2008，孟加拉隨機試驗
- 題錄：Zaman K 等 (2008). NEJM 359(15):1555-1564. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0708630>（PMID 18799552），摘要經 Europe PMC REST 取得。
- 摘要原文：「In this randomized study, we assigned 340 mothers to receive either inactivated influenza vaccine (influenza-vaccine group) or the 23-valent pneumococcal polysaccharide vaccine (control group). Mothers were interviewed weekly to assess illnesses until 24 weeks after birth.」
- 「Among infants of mothers who received influenza vaccine, there were fewer cases of laboratory-confirmed influenza than among infants in the control group (6 cases and 16 cases, respectively), with a vaccine effectiveness of 63% (95% confidence interval [CI], 5 to 85).」
- 「Among the mothers, there was a reduction in the rate of respiratory illness with fever of 36% (95% CI, 4 to 57).」
- 6 月齡以下不能打：「Inactivated influenza vaccine is recommended for pregnant women but is not licensed for infants younger than 6 months of age.」

## 3. Madhi 2014，南非隨機試驗
- 題錄：Madhi SA 等 (2014). NEJM 371(10):918-931. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1401480>（PMID 25184864）。同題還有 2014 年的讀者來信（10.1056/NEJMc1412050），不是這篇。
- 「We conducted two double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trials of trivalent inactivated influenza vaccine (IIV3) in South Africa」
- 「The attack rate for RT-PCR-confirmed influenza among both HIV-uninfected placebo recipients and their infants was 3.6%. The attack rates among HIV-uninfected IIV3 recipients and their infants were 1.8% and 1.9%, respectively, and the respective vaccine-efficacy rates were 50.4% (95% confidence interval [CI], 14.5 to 71.2) and 48.8% (95% CI, 11.6 to 70.4).」

## 4. 嬰兒的彙總：Nunes 2018、Jarvis 2020
- Nunes MC, Madhi SA (2018). Hum Vaccin Immunother 14(3):758-766. <https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2017.1345385>（PMID 28708952）。中國疾控 2025—2026、2026—2027 兩版指南都引了它。
  - 「Four randomized control trials and 3 observational studies reported on the prevention of laboratory-confirmed influenza infection in infants <6 months old. Maternal influenza vaccination was associated with a 48% [95% confidence interval (CI): 33 to 59] reduced risk of infants having laboratory-confirmed influenza infection. Four observational studies reported on the prevention of hospitalizations associated with laboratory-confirmed influenza infection and the pool estimate was 72% (95%CI: 39% to 87%).」
- Jarvis JR 等 (2020). Vaccine 38(7):1601-1613. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2019.12.056>（PMID 31932138）。
  - 「In a random effects meta-analysis of 2 RCTs including 5742 participants, maternal influenza vaccination was associated with an overall reduction of LCI in infants of 34% (95% confidence interval 15-50%). However, there was no effect of maternal influenza vaccination on ILI in infants ≤6 months old.」
  - 條目把這篇的「流感樣病沒有差別」寫進了收益欄，免得只報好的一面。

## 5. 孕婦住院：Thompson 2019
- 題錄：Thompson MG 等 (2019). Clin Infect Dis 68(9):1444-1453. <https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/ciy737>（PMID 30307490）。
- 「The Pregnancy Influenza Vaccine Effectiveness Network (PREVENT) consisted of public health or healthcare systems … in Australia, Canada (Alberta and Ontario), Israel, and the United States」；「pregnancies overlapped with local influenza seasons from 2010 through 2016」
- 「the adjusted overall IVE was 40% (95% confidence interval = 12%-59%) against influenza-associated hospitalization during pregnancy.」
- 中國疾控 2026—2027 指南同樣寫「對住院的 VE 為 40%（95%CI: 12%—59%）[79]」，引的就是這篇。

## 6. 安全性
- Giles ML 等 (2019). Hum Vaccin Immunother 15(3):687-699. <https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2018.1540807>（PMID 30380986）。
  - 「After removal of duplicates and full text eligibility assessment, 40 studies remained. The aOR for PTB was 0.87 (0.78-0.96), for LBW 0.82 (0.76-0.89), congenital abnormality 1.03 (0.99-1.07), SGA 0.99 (0.94-1.04) and stillbirth 0.84 (0.65-1.08).」
  - 這篇的結局裡有流產（fetal death 含 spontaneous abortion），但摘要沒給流產的合併數字，所以流產另引 Donahue 2019。
- Donahue JG 等 (2019). Vaccine 37(44):6673-6681. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2019.09.035>（PMID 31540812）。
  - 「We conducted a case-control study over three influenza seasons (2012-13, 2013-14, 2014-15) in the Vaccine Safety Datalink (VSD).」
  - 「Among 627 matched pairs vaccinated in the previous season, no association was found between vaccination in the 1-28 day risk window and SAB (adjusted odds ratio (aOR) 0.9; 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.6-1.5).」
  - 「These findings lend support to current recommendations for influenza vaccination at any time during pregnancy, including the first trimester.」
- 反方 Donahue JG 等 (2017). Vaccine 35(40):5314-5322. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2017.06.069>（PMID 28917295）。
  - 「The overall adjusted odds ratio (aOR) was 2.0 (95% CI, 1.1-3.6) for vaccine receipt in the 28-day exposure window」
  - 「Among women who received pH1N1-containing vaccine in the previous influenza season, the aOR in the 1-28days was 7.7 (95% CI 2.2-27.3); the aOR was 1.3 (95% CI 0.7-2.7) among women not vaccinated in the previous season.」
  - 「This study does not and cannot establish a causal relationship between repeated influenza vaccination and SAB, but further research is warranted.」
  - 2019 年那篇開頭就寫明是回應它：「A recent study reported an association between inactivated influenza vaccine (IIV) and spontaneous abortion (SAB), but only among women who had also been vaccinated in the previous influenza season.」所以條目標「爭議」，備註先寫 2017 年的信號，再寫複查沒重現。

## 7. 中國疾控中心《中國流感疫苗預防接種技術指南（2026—2027）》
- 發佈頁：<https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkyj/mygh02/jswj2026/202609/t20260902_1839739.html>（2026-09-02，無頭 Chrome 取得）。全文 PDF：<https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkyj/mygh02/jswj2026/202609/P020260902609797521311.pdf>（39 頁，pdftotext 能逐字抽中文）。2025—2026 版（<https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkts/202511/t20251103_313229.html>）也核過，孕婦部分措辭基本相同，條目引現行的 2026—2027 版。
- 優先人群：「（八）孕婦：世界衛生組織、部分國家和地區已將孕婦列為建議優先接種的人群。目前我國已有多款疫苗說明書不再將孕婦作為禁忌人群，且"建議與醫生共同進行獲益/風險評估後決定"。……本指南建議孕婦可接種疫苗說明書中未把孕婦列為接種禁忌的流感疫苗。」
  - 「多款」不再列為禁忌，意味著還有的說明書仍列為禁忌，所以成本欄和備註寫「挑說明書沒把孕婦列為禁忌的那一款」。
- 任何階段：「孕婦在孕期的任何階段接種流感疫苗均可以為 6 月齡以下嬰兒提供保護，孕晚期接種效果更好。」後面緊跟的數字：「美國一項病例對照研究結果顯示，母親孕晚期接種針對嬰兒流感相關住院的 VE 為 52%（95%CI:30%—68%），孕早期或孕中期接種 VE 為 17%（95%CI:-15%—40%）」。這個美國研究的原文沒取，條目只寫「孕晚期效果更好」，不寫這兩個數。
- 減毒活疫苗：「以下人群禁止接種 LAIV：……④孕婦」；「LAIV3 適用於 3—17 歲人群，每劑次 0.2 mL」；「LAIV 的接種採用鼻內噴霧法，嚴禁注射。」
- 接種時間：「通常在 11 月份左右進入冬春季流感流行季，建議在 9—10 月完成接種；即使此時間段內未完成接種，整個流行季也都可以接種。」
- 嬰兒家人：優先人群「（六）6 月齡以下嬰兒的家庭成員和看護人員」。疫苗適用年齡「可用於 6 月齡及以上人群」，所以備註寫孩子不滿 6 個月不能打。
- 指南還寫了孕婦接種與子癇前期、流產、胎兒死亡風險更低（HR 0.76、0.61、0.50 等），出自觀察研究、原文沒取，條目不用。

## 證據等級與收益量級
- 證據等級 A：兩項隨機試驗（Zaman、Madhi）加彙總分析，數字具體。標「爭議」是因為 Donahue 2017 的流產信號，反方證據和後續複查都寫在備註。
- 收益量級「大」：口徑是死亡率（含健康終點）。本條沒有死亡率的數字，終點是化驗確診的流感和因流感住院，這是健康終點，不是替代指標。按閾值機械套：孩子確診流感降 48%（彙總）到 63%（試驗），因流感住院降 72%，孕婦因流感住院降 40%，都 ≥20%，定「大」。這和第 1 節第 20 條同口徑同檔。保守的一端是 Jarvis 只合算兩項試驗得到的 34%，仍 ≥20%。
- 受益人：第 ① 檔你自己和第 ② 檔孩子。

## 沒有采用的
- CDC 的孕婦流感疫苗頁沒有單獨抓：WHO 和中國疾控指南已能逐字核對同樣的意思。
- 中國疾控指南里孕婦流感「死亡（OR=3.62）」：和 WHO 原文矛盾（WHO 說死亡 OR 1，不顯著），沒核到出處，不用。
