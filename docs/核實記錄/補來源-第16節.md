## 第 3 條（複查按醫生給的間隔做，把每次的指標記在同一個本子上）：C → B，收益量級 中 → 小

查過的地方：
- Europe PMC 檢索 `TITLE:"Self-monitoring of blood pressure in hypertension: A systematic review and individual patient data meta-analysis"`，命中 PMID 28926573。
- Europe PMC 檢索患者持有記錄（patient-held records）的系統綜述，命中四篇：Alsalem 2026 BMJ Open（只看用藥差錯的發現，終點不是健康結局）、Joseph 2021 BMJ Open（原文「no studies reported on health outcomes after using PHRs」）、Sartain 2015（質性研究）、Schoevers 2009。四篇都沒有看健康結局的數字，所以「記在一個本子上」本身沒有可引的效果證據，備註裡寫明瞭這一點。
- gov.cn 政策文件庫（Invoke-WebRequest，searchfield=title；curl 直連該接口這次一律返回「沒有找到相關結果」，換 PowerShell 才正常）檢索「檢查檢驗結果互認管理辦法」，命中國衛醫發〔2022〕6 號。
- 「國家基本公共衛生服務規範」在政策庫按標題檢索無結果，沒再追；條目寫的是「按醫生給的間隔」，不需要具體隨訪次數。

關鍵原文：
- Tucker 2017，<https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002389>：「Data for the primary outcomes … were available from 15/19 possible studies (7,138/8,292 [86%] of randomised participants). Overall, self-monitoring was associated with reduced clinic systolic blood pressure (sBP) compared to usual care at 12 months (-3.2 mmHg, [95% CI -4.9, -1.6 mmHg]). However, this effect was strongly influenced by the intensity of co-intervention ranging from no effect with self-monitoring alone (-1.0 mmHg [-3.3, 1.2]), to a 6.1 mmHg (-9.0, -3.2) reduction when monitoring was combined with intensive support.」結論：「Self-monitoring alone is not associated with lower BP or better control, but in conjunction with co-interventions (including systematic medication titration by doctors, pharmacists, or patients; education; or lifestyle counselling) leads to clinically significant BP reduction」。
- 國衛醫發〔2022〕6 號，<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-02/19/content_5674575.htm>：第十三條「醫療機構檢查檢驗結果互認標誌統一為HR……如：“全國HR”“京津冀HR”」；第十七條「對於患者提供的已有檢查檢驗結果符合互認條件、滿足診療需要的，醫療機構及其醫務人員不得重複進行檢查檢驗。」第十九條列了可以重查的六種情形（病情變化、結果變化快、手術輸血等重大措施前、急診急救、司法傷殘病退鑑定、其他）。

為什麼升 B、不升 A：Tucker 是隨機試驗的個體數據彙總，本身夠 A，但只覆蓋高血壓一種病、終點只到血壓，和條目「複查加記錄」是間接對應。按 CLAUDE.md 死亡率口徑「只有替代終點 = 小」，收益量級由原來憑感覺定的「中」機械改成「小」。互認辦法給的是換醫院省錢那一半的依據，不改變口徑。

說人話改寫：原來那句「記日期、指標、藥和劑量」留在備註，說人話改成收益欄裡有依據的三件事：只量不調藥沒用、帶給醫生調藥才有用、帶互認標誌的結果醫院不該讓重做。

## 第 4 條（別為了試偏方、保健品停掉正規治療）：C → B，收益量級維持 大

查過的地方：
- Europe PMC 檢索兩篇 Johnson 2018 的標題，拿到摘要原文；Crossref 核了 JNCI 那篇的卷期頁（110(1):121-124），Europe PMC 給了 JAMA Oncology 那篇的卷期頁（4(10):1375-1381，PMC6233773）。
- samr.gov.cn 發佈會頁（第 6 節第 10 條已在用）用無頭 Chrome 重抓一次，確認原句仍在。
- NCCIH 聖約翰草頁面，curl 帶代理取到，頁尾「Last Updated: May 2025」。

關鍵原文：
- Johnson 2018 JNCI，<https://doi.org/10.1093/jnci/djx145>：「We identified 281 patients with nonmetastatic breast, prostate, lung, or colorectal cancer who chose AM, administered as sole anticancer treatment among patients who did not receive conventional cancer treatment (CCT), defined as chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgery, and/or hormone therapy.」「AM use was independently associated with greater risk of death compared with CCT overall (hazard ratio [HR] = 2.50, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 1.88 to 3.27) and in subgroups with breast (HR = 5.68, 95% CI = 3.22 to 10.04), lung (HR = 2.17, 95% CI = 1.42 to 3.32), and colorectal cancer (HR = 4.57, 95% CI = 1.66 to 12.61).」摘要沒給前列腺癌亞組的數字，條目裡也就沒寫。
- Johnson 2018 JAMA Oncology，<https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2018.2487>：「Patients who chose CM did not have a longer delay to initiation of CCT but had higher refusal rates of surgery (7.0% [18 of 258] vs 0.1% [1 of 1031]; P < .001), chemotherapy (34.1% [88 of 258] vs 3.2% [33 of 1032]; P < .001), radiotherapy (53.0% [106 of 200] vs 2.3% [16 of 711]; P < .001), and hormone therapy (33.7% [87 of 258] vs 2.8% [29 of 1032]; P < .001). Use of CM was associated with poorer 5-year overall survival compared with no CM (82.2% [95% CI, 76.0%-87.0%] vs 86.6% [95% CI, 84.0%-88.9%]; P = .001) and was independently associated with greater risk of death (hazard ratio, 2.08; 95% CI, 1.50-2.90) … there was no significant association between CM and survival once treatment delay or refusal was included in the model (hazard ratio, 1.39; 95% CI, 0.83-2.33).」「The results suggest that mortality risk associated with CM was mediated by the refusal of CCT.」
- 市場監管總局發佈會，<https://www.samr.gov.cn/tssps/sjdt/tpxw/art/2023/art_4b658b824b1b4b0ba57c09a56cc93aad.html>：「《指南》提出，在標籤上標註“保健食品不是藥物，不能代替藥物治療疾病”警示語」。
- NCCIH，<https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/st-johns-wort>：「St. John’s wort can weaken the effects of many medicines, including crucially important medicines such as: … Cyclosporine, which prevents the body from rejecting transplanted organs … Warfarin, an anticoagulant (blood thinner)」。原說人話和備註裡「影響抗凝藥、抗排異藥在血裡的濃度」沒有出處，改成按這句寫的「減弱作用」，並挪進收益欄，說人話才有依據。

為什麼升 B、不升 A：兩篇都是美國國家癌症數據庫的回顧性跟蹤數據，暴露組只有兩三百人，只覆蓋癌症；沒有分組對照，殘餘混雜不能排除。數字明確、方向一致，夠 B。沒找到方向相反的研究，不標爭議。收益量級：HR 2.50 遠超 20%，按閾值仍是「大」。
