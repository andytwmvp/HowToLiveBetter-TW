## 1. Kramer 2019 薈萃
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.119.005554>（PMID 31592726，摘要經 Europe PMC 取得）
- 原文：「Ten studies were included yielding data from 3 837 005 participants (530 515 events; mean follow-up 10.1 years). Dog ownership was associated with a 24% risk reduction for all-cause mortality as compared to nonownership (relative risk, 0.76; 95% CI, 0.67-0.86)」；「in individuals with prior coronary events, living in a home with a dog was associated with an even more pronounced risk reduction for all-cause mortality (relative risk, 0.35; 95% CI, 0.17-0.69; I2, 0%)」；「dog ownership conferred a 31% risk reduction for cardiovascular death (relative risk, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.67-0.71; I2, 5.1%)」。
- 作者自承：「A possible limitation was that the analyses were not adjusted for confounders.」條目「這個彙總沒有扣除其他因素」即出自這句。
- 同刊有兩封讀者來信（Simonato 和 Altmayer；Owen、Bauman、Torske）和作者回復，摘要都沒有正文，沒引。

## 2. Mubanga 2017 瑞典全國登記
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-16118-6>（全文 PMC5693989）
- 原文（摘要）：「register-based prospective nation-wide cohort (n = 3,432,153) with up to 12 years of follow-up」；「In single- and multiple-person households, dog ownership (13.1%) was associated with lower risk of death, HR 0.67 (95% CI, 0.65-0.69) and 0.89 (0.87-0.91), respectively」；「Further analysis in the Twin Register could not replicate the reduced risk of CVD or death」；Twin Register「Self-reported health and lifestyle habits were available for 34,202 participants」。
- 校正因素（全文）：「The multivariable-adjusted model included sex, country of birth, marital status, presence of children in the home, area of residence, population density, age-adjusted income and latitude.」條目「扣除了收入、婚姻等因素」據此。
- 作者自承反向因果（全文）：「individuals with disabilities or comorbidity may be less likely to own a dog, and may be at higher risk for CVD and death」。備註「有病或殘疾的人可能本來就不太會養狗」據此。

## 3. Mubanga 2019 心梗、中風之後
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.118.005342>（PMID 31592725）
- 原文：「adjusted hazard ratio (HR) of 0.67 (95% CI, 0.61 to 0.75) for those who lived alone, and HR of 0.85 (95% CI, 0.80 to 0.90) for those living with a partner or a child」；「after an ischemic stroke … adjusted HR of 0.73 (95% CI, 0.66 to 0.80) for those who lived alone and HR of 0.88 (95% CI, 0.83 to 0.93) for those living with a partner or a child」；作者：「there are also unmeasured confounders such as smoking that prevent us from drawing conclusions regarding a possible causal effect」。

## 4. 反方：Ding 2018 英國
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amepre.2017.09.012>（PMID 29241716）
- 原文：「Data from six separate cohorts … of the Health Survey for England were pooled」「Participants were 59,352 adults」「mean 11.5, SD=3.8 years」；「In the fully adjusted models, there was no statistically significant association between dog ownership and mortality outcomes (hazard ratio=1.03, 95% CI=0.98, 1.09, for all-cause mortality; and hazard ratio=1.07, 95% CI=0.96, 1.18, for cardiovascular disease mortality)」。

## 5. 反方：Torske 2017 挪威 HUNT
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179832>（PMC5491039）
- 原文：「The median follow-up time was 18.5 years」；「dog owners were no more physically active than non-dog owners」；「Dog owners … had virtually the same hazard of dying as non-dog owners (Hazard ratio 1.00, 95% CI 0.91-1.09)」。

## 6. Taniguchi 2024 澳大利亞 HILDA（養貓等其他寵物）
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305546>（PMC11324118）
- 原文：「The study sample included 15,735 participants」「During the 4-year follow-up period」；「The odds ratios (ORs) for all-cause mortality were 0.77 (95%CI: 0.59-0.99) for dog owners compared to non-pet owners」；「The Sobel test showed a partial mediating effect of physical activity level」；「Ownership of cats, birds, fish, and others showed no clear association with mortality」。

## 7. Mubanga 2019 BMJ Open（血壓血脂）
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023447>（PMC6429732）
- 原文：「slightly higher likelihood of initiating antihypertensive (HR, 1.02; 95% CI, 1.01 to 1.03) and lipid-lowering treatment (HR, 1.02; 95% CI, 1.01 to 1.04) in dog owners」；「implying that the previously reported lower risk of cardiovascular mortality among dog owners in this cohort is not explained by reduced hypertension and dyslipidaemia」。備註只寫結論，沒寫數字。

## 8. 美國心臟協會 2013 聲明
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0b013e31829201e1>。出版社頁被 Cloudflare 擋（curl 和無頭 Chrome 都只拿到質詢頁），全文從 web.archive.org 2025-02-18 的存檔取得：<http://web.archive.org/web/20250218075849/https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/full/10.1161/CIR.0b013e31829201e1>。
- 原文：「1. Pet ownership, particularly dog ownership, may be reasonable for reduction in CVD risk (Class IIb; Level of Evidence B). 2. Pet adoption, rescue, or purchase should not be done for the primary purpose of reducing CVD risk (Class III; Level of Evidence C).」
- 原文：「Methodological issues in many studies of pet ownership and CVD include modest numbers of subjects, confounding factors (eg, sociodemographics, comorbid medical conditions, and unidentified differences between those who choose to own or not own pets) … and (understandably) lack of randomized data.」
- 條目把 Class IIb 寫成「最弱一檔的推薦」：IIb 是該體系裡有推薦意義的最低一檔，Class III 是不推薦。

## 沒有收的
- El-Qushayri 2020 J Cardiovasc Med 薈萃（<https://doi.org/10.2459/jcm.0000000000000920>）：結論和 Kramer 相反，養狗與總死亡、心血管死亡都無顯著關聯，養貓反而心血管死亡低（校正 HR 0.79，0.63–0.99）、總死亡不變（1.04）；作者結尾又「推薦養寵物」，推薦和數據對不上，摘要的納入口徑也混了寵物整體和分物種，沒引，只在這裡記一筆。
- Sørensen 2018 丹麥（<https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/cky164>）：短報告，病例對照設計，結論和瑞典一致（獨居的人關聯更強），沒有增加新信息。
- Feng 2026 Gerontologist 綜述：只做計數（幾項正、幾項負），沒有合併效應量。
- 養狗的費用、寵物保險：國內沒有官方統計，按用戶定的口徑不寫；保險原則在第 5 節第 39 條。
- 遛狗的具體時長、步數：沒有找到能逐字核對的數字，成本欄只寫方向。

## 收益量級與證據等級
- 收益「大」：按死亡率口徑閾值機械套，Kramer 彙總 RR 0.76，降幅 24%，≥20%。但這個數沒有校正混雜；校正後的英國、挪威研究為零，備註寫明。
- 證據等級 B 而不是 A：雖有薈萃和大型登記研究，但唯一能合併出的數字沒扣混雜，扣了混雜的幾項結果互相打架，數字不可當真，按「難以量化」給 B，並標爭議。
- 成本：錢=多（長期支出）、時間=多（每天遛狗）、毅力=是（要天天管到狗老死，狗能活多少年沒找來源，條目裡沒寫年數）。三項都按方向定，沒有具體金額。
