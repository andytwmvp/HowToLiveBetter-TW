# 追加：第 3 節第 27 條，打鼾憋氣、白天犯困的去查睡眠呼吸暫停，中重度的戴呼吸機

2026-10-14。主任務按「對照各節主要風險補條目」派來的候選。

## 查重
- 全書 grep「呼吸暫停|打鼾|CPAP|呼吸機」：第 2 節第 37 條（午睡）備註只說「打呼嚕和睡覺憋氣要查睡眠呼吸暫停」，第 2 節第 3 條（睡夠 7 小時）備註把它列為睡得長的原因之一，第 3 節第 26 條（長期失眠）備註說規範要求先排除它。三處都是順帶一句，沒有「怎麼查、查出來怎麼治、治了有什麼用」，不算重複。
- 第 3 節第 26 條備註原來寫「怎麼查見第 2 節第 37 條」，可那條並沒寫怎麼查，改成指向本條。
- 第 1 節第 9 條（困了就停車歇）管的是開車當下，本條管犯困的病因，兩邊互相指路。

## 四問
1. 會碰上：2019 年估算中國 30 到 69 歲約 1.76 億人有這個病。
2. 普遍不知道：多數人把打鼾當睡得香，不知道能查、能治。
3. 動作是新的：去做睡眠監測、戴呼吸機，全書沒有。
4. 有原始來源：美國睡眠醫學會指南和系統綜述、NEJM 隨機試驗、JAMA 薈萃。

## 1. Kapur 2017，AASM 診斷指南
- <https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.6506>，全文 <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5337595/>（2026-10-14 帶代理取到）
- 「An increased risk of moderate to severe OSA is indicated by the presence of excessive daytime sleepiness and at least two of the following three criteria: habitual loud snoring, witnessed apnea or gasping or choking, or diagnosed hypertension.」
- 「excessive daytime sleepiness occurring on most days」
- 「We recommend that clinical tools, questionnaires and prediction algorithms not be used to diagnose OSA in adults, in the absence of polysomnography or home sleep apnea testing. (STRONG)」
- 「We recommend that polysomnography, or home sleep apnea testing with a technically adequate device, be used for the diagnosis of OSA in uncomplicated adult patients ...」（條目裡「把儀器借回家戴著睡」的依據）
- 診斷標準：「ICSD-3 defines OSA as a PSG-determined obstructive respiratory disturbance index (RDI) ≥ 5 events/h associated with the typical symptoms of OSA ... or an obstructive RDI ≥ 15 events/h (even in the absence of symptoms).」

## 2. Patil 2019，AASM 系統綜述
- <https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.7638>，全文 PMC6374080
- 睏倦：「A meta-analysis of 38 RCTs ... demonstrated a clinically significant reduction in self-reported sleepiness of −2.4 points in the ESS score (95% CI: −2.8 to −1.9 points) in participants on PAP compared to controls」
- 不困的人：「A sub-analysis of the 5 studies ... recruiting only non-sleepy participants with OSA demonstrated an ESS reduction of −1.0 points (95% CI: −0.7 to −1.4 points) that the TF judged to not be clinically significant.」
- 車禍：「Meta-analyses of the 10 non-randomized studies ... comparing participants with OSA before and after CPAP treatment demonstrated a mean crash rate risk ratio of 0.3 (95% CI: 0.2 to 0.4)」；「The quality of evidence for MVC ranged from low to moderate.」
- ESS 滿分 24 分是量表的通用設定，原文沒寫，條目只作解釋用。

## 3. Tregear 2010，Sleep
- <https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.10.1373>，Europe PMC 摘要
- 「A meta-analysis of 9 observational studies examining crash risk of drivers with OSA pre- vs. post-CPAP found a significant risk reduction following treatment (risk ratio = 0.278, 95% CI: 0.22 to 0.35; P < 0.001).」
- 「daytime sleepiness improves significantly following a single night of treatment, and simulated driving performance improves significantly within 2 to 7 days of CPAP treatment.」

## 4. Tregear 2009，J Clin Sleep Med
- PMID 20465027，PMC2792976
- 「The mean crash-rate ratio associated with OSA is likely to fall within the range of 1.21 to 4.89.」

## 5. McEvoy 2016，SAVE，NEJM 375(10):919-931
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1606599>，摘要
- 「randomly assigned 2717 eligible adults between 45 and 75 years of age who had moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea and coronary or cerebrovascular disease」
- 「Most of the participants were men who had moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea and minimal sleepiness.」
- 「the mean duration of adherence to CPAP therapy was 3.3 hours per night」
- 「After a mean follow-up of 3.7 years, a primary end-point event had occurred in 229 participants in the CPAP group (17.0%) and in 207 participants in the usual-care group (15.4%) (hazard ratio with CPAP, 1.10; 95% confidence interval, 0.91 to 1.32; P=0.34).」
- 「CPAP significantly reduced snoring and daytime sleepiness and improved health-related quality of life and mood.」

## 6. Yu 2017，JAMA 318(2):156-166

## 等級與量級
- 證據等級 A：睏倦改善來自 38 項隨機試驗。標「爭議」：心血管和總死亡率在隨機試驗和薈萃裡都沒看出差別；車禍數字只來自前後對比。
- 收益「中」是判斷：口徑是時間（精力），睏倦分數降 2.4 分有臨床意義，但沒有研究量出每天多出幾小時清醒時間，不夠「每天小時級」。車禍少約七成屬於死亡率口徑，不跨口徑換算。
- 成本：沒查到睡眠監測和呼吸機的官方價格，成本欄只寫「一筆不小的錢」，沒寫數字；錢=多按呼吸機是一次性大件定。
- 沒加人群標籤：7 個值裡沒有對應的。
