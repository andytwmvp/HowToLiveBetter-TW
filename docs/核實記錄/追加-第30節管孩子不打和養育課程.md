# 追加：第 30 節第 16、17 條，管孩子不打不罵、去上教具體做法的家長課

2026-10-01。讀者建議補「家庭關係處理」，理由是這些對情緒影響很大。定下的原則是不新開一節，只補有硬證據的條目，不說教，不寫話術。本輪只在第 30 節節末追加兩條，沒有插在中間，免得後面的條號順延。節首引言裡講口徑的那句同步改了：原來寫「前八條和最後兩條」算死亡和身體損害，第 14 條（打遊戲）加進來以後已經不準，現在逐條列出。

## 沒有照收的部分

- 「怎麼和孩子說話」「吵架後怎麼修復」這類話術：沒有能套閾值的原始數字，按原則不寫。
- 夫妻關係、婆媳關係：不在第 30 節的範圍內。夫妻吵架的健康賬已在第 10 節。
- 孩子被家裡人打以後的報警和保護令：第 8 節第 43 條已寫，本輪只指路，不重複。

## 第 16 條：不打、不吼罵羞辱

### 1. Gershoff & Grogan-Kaylor 2016 打屁股的薈萃分析
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000191>，全文按 PMC 作者稿核對：<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7992110/>
- 題錄（Europe PMC）：Journal of Family Psychology 30(4):453–469，PMID 27055181。
- 原文（摘要）：「Meta-analyses focused specifically on spanking were conducted on a total of 111 unique effect sizes representing 160,927 children. Thirteen of 17 mean effect sizes were significantly different from zero and all indicated a link between spanking and increased risk for detrimental child outcomes. Effect sizes did not substantially differ between spanking and physical abuse or by study design characteristics.」
- 原文（定義）：「we define as hitting a child on their buttocks or extremities using an open hand」
- 原文（納入）：「Only 75 studies met all four criteria and were retained for the meta-analyses.」
- 表 2 逐項（d，95% CI）：Immediate defiance .14（−.19 .47，K=5，不顯著）；Child aggression .37（.13 .61）；Child antisocial behavior .39（.24 .53）；Child externalizing behavior problems .41（.32 .50）；Child internalizing behavior problems .24（.13 .35）；Child mental health problems .53（.42 .64）；Negative parent–child relationship .51（.36 .66）；Impaired cognitive ability .17（.01 .32）；Low self-esteem .15（.04 .26）；Victim of physical abuse .64；Adult antisocial behavior .36（.06 .65）；Adult mental health problems .24（.09 .40）；Overall effect size .33（.29 .38）。
- 被打的那一行原表印的可信範圍是「.39 1.74」，上下不對稱，疑似排印錯誤，所以條目裡只寫了 0.64，不寫範圍。
- 原文（侷限）：「As most of the included studies were correlational or retrospective (72%), causal links between spanking and child outcomes cannot be established by these meta-analyses.」

### 2. Heilmann 等 2021 Lancet 綜述
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)00582-1>，全文按 PMC 作者稿核對：<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8612122/>
- 題錄：Lancet 398(10297):355–364。
- 原文：「This narrative review summarises the findings of 69 prospective longitudinal studies」；「First, physical punishment consistently predicts increases in child behaviour problems over time. Second, physical punishment is not associated with positive outcomes over time.」；「Seven studies measured the relationship between frequency of physical punishment and level of the outcome variable. Five of these studies found evidence of a dose–response effect」。

### 3. Wang & Kenny 2014 吼罵的跟蹤研究
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12143>，全文：<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3875601/>
- 原文（摘要）：「Data were from a sample of 976 two-parent families」；「Mothers' and fathers' harsh verbal discipline at age 13 predicted an increase in adolescent conduct problems and depressive symptoms between ages 13 and 14. A child effect was also present, with adolescent misconduct at age 13 predicting increases in mothers' and fathers' harsh verbal discipline between ages 13 and 14. Furthermore, maternal and paternal warmth did not moderate the longitudinal associations」。
- 原文（測量）：「a) shouted, yelled, or screamed at the child, b) swore or cursed at the child, and c) called the child dumb or lazy or some other name like that」。條目裡的「吼叫、罵髒話、罵孩子笨或懶」按這三項翻譯。
- 跟蹤了多久：只核到 13 歲和 14 歲兩個時間點之間的關聯，條目裡沒寫「跟蹤了幾年」。

### 4. 反方：Ferguson 2013
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2012.11.002>，Clinical Psychology Review 33(1):196–208。
- 原文（摘要）：「when better controlled partial r coefficients (pr) were examined, results were statistically significant but trivial (at or below pr = .10) for externalizing (.07 for spanking, .08 for CP) and internalizing behaviors (.10 for spanking, insufficient studies for CP)」；「It is concluded that the impact of spanking and CP on the negative outcomes evaluated here ... are minimal.」
- 注意這裡的 0.07、0.10 是偏相關係數，不是 d。條目裡寫的是「關聯只剩 0.07……0.10」，沒有標成 d。

### 5. 反方：Larzelere & Kuhn 2005
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1007/s10567-005-2340-z>，Clinical Child and Family Psychology Review 8(1):1–37。
- 原文（摘要）：「effect sizes significantly favored conditional spanking over 10 of 13 alternative disciplinary tactics for reducing child noncompliance or antisocial behavior. Customary physical punishment yielded effect sizes equal to alternative tactics, except for one large study favoring physical punishment. Only overly severe or predominant use of physical punishment compared unfavorably with alternative disciplinary tactics.」
- 「有條件地打」的具體定義只在全文裡，全文沒取到（Springer），所以條目裡沒解釋它指什麼，只照摘要寫結論。

### 6. 法條（flk 國家法律法規數據庫，sxx=3 現行有效，docx 逐字核對）
- 反家庭暴力法（2015-12-27 公佈，2016-03-01 施行）<https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7f9d60889>
  - 第二條：「本法所稱家庭暴力，是指家庭成員之間以毆打、捆綁、殘害、限制人身自由以及經常性謾罵、恐嚇等方式實施的身體、精神等侵害行為。」
  - 第十二條：「未成年人的監護人應當以文明的方式進行家庭教育，依法履行監護和教育職責，不得實施家庭暴力。」
- 未成年人保護法（2024-04-26 修正，現行）<https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081818f197cf001905e567af635a0>
  - 第十七條：「未成年人的父母或者其他監護人不得實施下列行為：（一）虐待、遺棄、非法送養未成年人或者對未成年人實施家庭暴力」
  - 第十一條第一款：「任何組織或者個人發現不利於未成年人身心健康或者侵犯未成年人合法權益的情形，都有權勸阻、制止或者向公安、民政、教育等有關部門提出檢舉、控告。」
  - 第一百一十八條：「未成年人的父母或者其他監護人不依法履行監護職責或者侵犯未成年人合法權益的，由其居住地的居民委員會、村民委員會予以勸誡、制止；情節嚴重的，居民委員會、村民委員會應當及時向公安機關報告。公安機關接到報告或者公安機關、人民檢察院、人民法院在辦理案件過程中發現未成年人的父母或者其他監護人存在上述情形的，應當予以訓誡，並可以責令其接受家庭教育指導。」
  - 第二十七條禁止的「體罰、變相體罰」，主語是「學校、幼兒園的教職員工」，不是父母。所以條目裡沒有寫「父母體罰違法」，只寫了父母「不得實施家庭暴力」。
- 家庭教育促進法（2021-10-23 公佈，2022-01-01 施行）<https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817cac3b2d017cac5a6c6f0109>
  - 第二十三條：「未成年人的父母或者其他監護人不得因性別、身體狀況、智力等歧視未成年人，不得實施家庭暴力……」
  - 第五十三條：「未成年人的父母或者其他監護人在家庭教育過程中對未成年人實施家庭暴力的，依照《中華人民共和國未成年人保護法》、《中華人民共和國反家庭暴力法》等法律的規定追究法律責任。」
- 法條管到什麼程度：反家暴法點名的是「毆打」和「經常性」的謾罵、恐嚇。偶爾打一下、吼一句算不算，條文沒有明說，條目備註照這個寫，沒有寫成「打一下就違法」。

### 證據等級與收益量級
- 證據等級 A，備註以「爭議」開頭：有大型薈萃給出具體效應量，但底下幾乎全是觀察性研究，且有 Ferguson 和 Larzelere 兩篇反方薈萃。
- 收益「中」、口徑「死亡率」（按全書口徑，死亡率含健康終點；這裡的終點是孩子的心理健康和行為問題）。這類效應量是 d，不是相對風險，沒法按「降幅 ≥20% 為大」的閾值機械套，所以憑判斷定。憑的是：總的 d = 0.33，按術語表是小到中；心理健康問題那一項 0.53，算中等；但扣掉孩子原來的行為之後，關聯在 0.10 以下。兩頭折中取「中」。偏保守的讀法會定「小」，留給主任務定奪。

## 第 17 條：去上教具體做法的家長課

### 1. Furlong 等 2012 Cochrane（CD008225.pub2，Europe PMC 查無更新版本，pub3 的 DOI 返回 404）
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD008225.pub2>，全文：<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12935172/>
- 原文（摘要）：「This review includes 13 trials (10 RCTs and three quasi-randomised trials)」；「Overall, there were 1078 participants」；「parent training produced a statistically significant reduction in child conduct problems, whether assessed by parents (standardised mean difference (SMD) -0.53; 95% confidence interval (CI) -0.72 to -0.34) or independently assessed (SMD -0.44; 95% CI -0.77 to -0.11). The intervention led to statistically significant improvements in parental mental health (SMD -0.36; 95% CI -0.52 to -0.20)」；「a statistically significant reduction in negative or harsh parenting practices according to both parent reports (SMD -0.77; 95% CI -0.96 to -0.59) and independent assessments (SMD -0.42; 95% CI -0.67 to -0.16)」；結論「in the short term」。
- 原文（課程形態）：「provided on a regular basis (for example, weekly or fortnightly) for at least three sessions of between one and two hours」；「may range from four ( Martin 2003 ) to 24 weekly group sessions」；「We also excluded education or information‐based training programmes (that is programmes that rely only on providing information or discussion, or both)」；「teach key behavioural principles and parenting skills (for example, play, praise, rewards, discipline) to parents and caregivers who then practise the skills」；「behaviour rehearsal (for example, role‐play); discussion; homework assignments」。
- 課名：檢索時搜了「Triple P Library, Incredible Years Library」和「Parent‐Child interaction therapy library」，納入研究裡有 Incredible Years BASIC，參考文獻裡有 Leung 2009 在華人家庭做的 PCIT。
- 摘要裡另有「每戶約 2500 美元」的成本效益數字，是英美的成本，和國內讀者無關，沒寫進條目。

### 2. Backhaus 等 2023 eClinicalMedicine
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2023.102003>，eClinicalMedicine 60:102003。
- 原文（摘要）：「We screened 44,411 records and included 346 RCTs. Sixty RCTs reported outcomes on physical or emotional violence. Trials were distributed across 22 countries (22% LMICs). Risk of bias was high for various domains. Outcome data ... was largely based on parent self-report.」；「immediately after the intervention (n = 42, k = 59; d = -0.46; 95% CI: -0.59, -0.33), at 1-6 months follow-up (n = 18, k = 31; d = -0.24; 95% CI: -0.37, -0.11) and at 7-24 months follow-up (n = 12, k = 19; d = -0.18; 95% CI: -0.34, -0.02), but effects were smaller over time.」
- 納入表裡有 Guo 2016（中國，Triple P，81 人，結局為體罰），檢索覆蓋了中文數據庫。

### 3. Guo, Morawska, Sanders 2016（上海）
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1177/0145445516644221>，Behavior Modification 40(6):825–851。
- 原文（摘要）：「Participants were 81 Chinese parents and their children in Shanghai, who were randomly allocated to an intervention group or wait-list control group.」；「parents in the intervention group reported significant improvements in child adjustment problems, parenting practices, parental adjustment, and parenting self-efficacy at post-assessment」；「All these effects were maintained at 6-month follow-up.」
- 摘要沒給效應量，條目裡只寫方向。

### 4. Wang & Zhang 2026（農村幼兒園）
- URL：<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chiabu.2026.107937>，Child Abuse & Neglect 173:107937。
- 原文（摘要）：「Twelve classes (191 caregivers) in one rural kindergarten were cluster-randomized 1:1 to either a self-directed online PLH-YC program (100 caregivers) or a book-reading control (91 caregivers).」；「Two weeks post-intervention, caregivers in the self-directed group reported a 33% reduction in overall child maltreatment (IRR = 0.67, p < 0.001)」；「At the three-month follow-up, reductions in child behavior problems became significant (d = -0.40, p < 0.001).」；「Parenting efficacy and parental depressive symptoms did not differ between groups at both time points.」
- 只有一所幼兒園、跟了三個月，所以條目裡只放在「國內也有試驗」那一段，不作為主證據。

### 5. 家庭教育促進法（同上鏈接，逐字核對）
- 第十八條：「未成年人的父母或者其他監護人應當樹立正確的家庭教育理念，自覺學習家庭教育知識……」
- 第二十五條：「省級以上人民政府應當組織有關部門統籌建設家庭教育信息化共享服務平臺，開設公益性網上家長學校和網絡課程，開通服務熱線，提供線上家庭教育指導服務。」
- 第三十一條：「家庭教育指導機構開展家庭教育指導服務活動，不得組織或者變相組織營利性教育培訓。」
- 第三十八條：「居民委員會、村民委員會可以依託城鄉社區公共服務設施，設立社區家長學校等家庭教育指導服務站點……」
- 第四十條：「中小學校、幼兒園可以採取建立家長學校等方式，針對不同年齡段未成年人的特點，定期組織公益性家庭教育指導服務和實踐活動……」
- 沒查到：國內公益家長學校用的是不是上面這類結構化課程。條目備註明寫「未必是這一類」，並給了挑課的兩個判據（連上幾周、練具體做法），判據取自 Cochrane 的納入和排除標準。

### 證據等級與收益量級
- 證據等級 A：Cochrane 合併了抽籤試驗，另一篇薈萃收了 60 項抽籤試驗，都給了具體數字。侷限（家長自評為主、偏倚風險高、效果隨時間變小）寫進收益欄和備註，沒有反方證據，所以不標爭議。
- 收益「中」、口徑「死亡率」（終點是孩子的行為問題和家長的心理健康）。效應量是 SMD，不能套相對降幅閾值，憑判斷定：孩子行為問題 −0.53、打罵 −0.46 都在「中等」附近，一兩年後降到 −0.18，所以取「中」。
- 成本標籤：錢=0（公益家長學校不收費；市面收費課不推薦），時間=中（每週一到兩小時、持續數週），毅力=些。
