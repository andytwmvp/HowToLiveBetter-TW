# 追加：第 8 節第 46 條，門窗裝好鎖、門外裝感應燈防入室盜竊（issue #78）

2026-10-05。issue #78 問「如何防身」，讀者想知道家裡備什麼防入室搶劫。查下來，能逐字核對、帶具體數字的只有英格蘭和威爾士犯罪調查（CSEW）上門窗鎖、燈和報警器與入室盜竊的關係，管的是盜竊，不是搶劫。條目據此老實寫成防盜，標題和說人話都寫明不管搶劫；人在家裡遇到人的處置指向第 8 節第 10 條（起了衝突先報警不動手）和第 11 條（躲不掉的侵害可以還手）。追加在節末，沒有插在中間。節首導覽把原「開車和養狗」塊改名為「開車、養狗和防盜」，掛上「門窗裝好鎖（第 46 條）」。

## 等級和量級怎麼定的

- 證據等級 B：數字具體，但都來自同一個研究團隊、同一套調查數據，而且是比較裝了和沒裝的人家（作者把它叫「準自然實驗」），不是隨機試驗或薈萃分析，夠不上 A。
- 標「爭議」：報警器那一半，同一批作者用 1992—1996 年數據算出的是加裝報警器降低風險，2008—2012 年才反過來，作者自己說要謹慎看待；Tilley 2015 綜述的竊賊問卷研究裡，「about 60 per cent indicated that the presence of an alarm would deter」。
- 口徑金錢。收益欄沒有金額，閾值套不上，憑判斷記「中」：相對降幅很大（十幾到幾十倍），但入室盜竊的基礎發生率低（CSEW 2012/13 年為 2.1% 的家庭被盜），一年下來省的期望金額有限；一次被盜的損失多在數百到數千元這一檔的判斷沒有找到國內官方統計，沒寫進條目。
- 成本欄「幾十到幾百元」「防盜門上千元」是市場常識，不是文獻數字。

## 1. Tseloni 等 (2017) Security Journal（主來源）

- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1057/sj.2014.30>，開放獲取 PDF：<https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1057/sj.2014.30.pdf>（pdftotext 逐字核對）
- 摘要：「It finds that, for individual devices, external lights and door double locks or deadlocks, are most effective but, counter-intuitively, burglar alarms and dummy alarms confer less protection than no security. … In particular, combinations with door and window locks plus external lights or security chains confer at least 20 times greater protection against burglary with entry than no security.」
- 樣本與 4.9%、36.6%：「The bottom row shows that there were 37 416 households in the sample, of which 2245 experienced burglary and 1356 experienced attempted burglary. The first row of the table shows that 1835 of all 37 416 households (4.9 per cent) reported no security devices. However, 821 of 2245 burgled households (36.6 per cent) reported no security」。數據年份是 2008/2009–2011/2012 CSEW，條目寫成「2008 到 2012 年間」。
- 門鎖加窗鎖 12.54：「households with window and door locks have an odds ratio with respect to burglary with entry of 0.59, which is 12.54 (7.46/0.59) times lower than no security. … Another way of expressing this is that window and door locks reduce the odds ratio of burglary with entry down to one-twelfth compared with no security.」（PDF 版面裡表 2 的列對不齊，0.59 按正文這句認。）
- 三樣組合 34.41：表 3 第二行「EWD 34.41**」；正文「the highest (roughly 34 and 14 SPFs for completed and attempted burglary, respectively) is offered by external lights, window and door locks (EWD)」。
- 四樣 49：表 3「EIWD 49.12**」；正文「lights (external and indoor) and locks (for windows and doors) (EIWD) that confers similar protection by a factor of 49」。
- 單裝：「external lights or door locks confer the highest protection (SPFs of 3 and 2.8, respectively, for burglary with entry) among all individual devices」。窗鎖：「When devices are used individually, window locks have the highest SPF followed by indoor sensor lights, but neither has statistically significant odds ratios … This suggests we cannot conclude with confidence that they confer greater than no security.」
- 報警器與假報警器：「Burglar alarms and dummy alarms appear to increase risk of attempted burglary. The SPFs of dummy alarm for attempts and burglar alarm for any type of burglary are less than 1 implying a counter-productive effect: having just a burglar alarm and no other form of security confers less protection than no security at all.」條目裡「掛假報警器盒子的，有人想闖進來沒闖成的幾率也更高」對應「dummy alarm for attempts」，即 attempted burglary（未遂入室）。子代理初稿寫成「被人撬門」，主任務抽查時改掉：原文沒說是撬門。
- 裝五樣不比三樣強：「From a narrow viewpoint of burglary security, households, landlords and crime and safety partnerships should not even contemplate installing five devices since such configurations are less effective against burglary with entry than the above triplets and quadruplets.」
- 門鎖的定義：設施清單原文「Door double locks or deadlocks」「External lights on a timer or sensor」。條目把 deadlock 解釋成「鎖舌要用鑰匙才能收回」。
- 租房：原文「Households in the rental sector also require the cooperation of landlords to make changes to their residence」，條目的租房提醒改引民法典第七百一十五條（見第 5 項）。
- 2.1%（只用於本記錄的量級判斷）：「The Crime Survey for England and Wales (CSEW) estimates that 2.1 per cent of households in England and Wales were burgled in 2012/2013」。

## 2. Tilley 等 (2015) Crime Prevention and Community Safety（報警器反方）

- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1057/cpcs.2014.17>，開放獲取 PDF：<https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1057/cpcs.2014.17.pdf>
- 摘要：「It finds that alarms have become associated with increased rather than decreased risk of burglary with entry. This counter-intuitive finding needs to be treated cautiously.」
- 早年數據相反：「For 1992–1996 the addition of alarms to otherwise similar configurations of security device, was associated with reduced risk of burglary with entry.」
- 作者的保留：「It is too early yet to derive that conclusion」。被偷過才裝的解釋見 Security Journal 那篇：「Another plausible explanation is that burglar alarms were fitted to homes that had been burgled before the CSEW reference period and therefore the respective households are repeat burglary victims, although the data cannot confirm this.」

## 3. Tseloni 等 (2017) Crime Science（撬鎖類盜竊下降、沒鎖的不降）

- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1186/s40163-017-0064-2>，全文 PMC7089640，經 Europe PMC fullTextXML 取得：<https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7089640/fullTextXML>
- 原文：「burglaries with forced entry per 1000 households dropped from 31 in 1993 to 7 in 2006/07, a fall of 77%, while unforced entry burglaries remained at 7 per 1000 households in the same period.」
- 撬鎖、沒鎖的定義：「forced entry, which involves forcing lock or window, breaking/cutting glass or breaking/removing door panel; unforced entry, which includes entering via doors or windows that were left open or unlocked」。
- WIDE 四樣組合 49 與上文一致：「properties fitted with the four ‘WIDE’ devices: window locks (W), indoor lights on a timer (I), door double or deadlocks (D) and external lights on a sensor (E) have an SPF of 49.」
