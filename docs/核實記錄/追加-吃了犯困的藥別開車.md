## 四問
1. 普通讀者會碰上嗎：會。藥監局點名的酚麻美敏、複方氨酚烷胺、氨咖黃敏、維C銀翹片都是家裡常備的感冒藥，開車的人很多。
2. 讀者是不是普遍不知道：感冒藥和開車之間的關係，多數人想不到；試驗還說自己覺得困不困預測不了開得好不好，這一點更不是常識。
3. 動作是新的嗎：是。本節第 1 條管的是對乙酰氨基酚重複，第 1 節第 9 條管的是疲勞和手機，都沒有這個動作。
4. 有官方原始來源嗎：有，國家藥監局兩份原文，見下。

## 3. Weiler JM 等 (2000)，Annals of Internal Medicine 132(5):354-363
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.7326/0003-4819-132-5-200003070-00004>（Europe PMC 摘要，PMID 10691585）
- 設計：「Randomized, double-blind, double-dummy, four-treatment, four-period crossover trial.」；「40 licensed drivers with seasonal allergic rhinitis who were 25 to 44 years of age.」
- 處理：「One dose of fexofenadine (60 mg), diphenhydramine (50 mg), alcohol (approximately 0.1% blood alcohol concentration), or placebo, given at weekly intervals before participants drove for 1 hour in the Iowa Driving Simulator.」
- 結果：「Participants had significantly better coherence after taking alcohol or fexofenadine than after taking diphenhydramine.」；「Lane keeping (steering instability and crossing the center line) was impaired after alcohol and diphenhydramine use compared with fexofenadine use.」；「Mean response time to the blocking vehicle was slowest after alcohol use (2.21 seconds) compared with fexofenadine use (1.95 seconds).」
- 結論：「Participants had similar performance when treated with fexofenadine or placebo.」；「diphenhydramine had a greater impact on driving than alcohol did. Drowsiness ratings were not a good predictor of impairment, suggesting that drivers cannot use drowsiness to indicate when they should not drive.」
- coherence 是「跟住前車變速的能力」，條目譯作「跟住前車車速的能力」。

# 沒寫：孩子發燒別用酒精擦身

想找的是「酒精擦身」這件事本身的原始來源，沒有取到，所以不寫，也不併入第 2 條。

## 查到的
- NICE NG143 Fever in under 5s，1.6.2：「Tepid sponging is not recommended for the treatment of fever. [2007]」<https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng143/chapter/Recommendations>（帶代理無頭 Chrome 取到）
- NHS High temperature (fever) in children：「do not undress your child or sponge them down to cool them – a high temperature is a natural and healthy response to infection」<https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/fever-in-children/>
- Meremikwu M, Oyo-Ita A (2003) Cochrane CD004264，Physical methods for treating fever in children：7 項試驗 467 人；兩項人人都吃了退燒藥的試驗裡，加物理降溫 1 小時退熱的比例更高（RR 11.8，CI 3.39 到 40.8）；發抖、起雞皮疙瘩更多（3 項，RR 5.09，CI 1.56 到 16.60）。結論「A few small studies demonstrate that tepid sponging helps to reduce fever in children.」<https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004264>
- 國家衛健委 2016 年科普《關於兒童發熱的防治知識》反過來寫「物理降溫（可採取溫水浴、退熱貼、冰敷等）」<https://www.nhc.gov.cn/yzygj/c100067/201602/edaec3dcd70b457ebc81be747a832119.shtml>

## 沒取到的
- AAP 2011 Sullivan & Farrar, Pediatrics 127(3):580-587（doi:10.1542/peds.2010-3852）：摘要沒有提擦身和酒精；全文在 publications.aap.org，帶代理無頭 Chrome 被 Cloudflare 驗證頁擋住，Semantic Scholar 標為 CLOSED，PMC 沒收。
- healthychildren.org（AAP 家長站）發熱頁：本輪返回錯誤頁。
- WHO Pocket book of hospital care for children（2013）第 10.3 節：只寫了輕穿衣、多喝水和退燒藥，沒有提擦身和酒精。
- 酒精經皮吸收中毒的兒童病例報告：Europe PMC 按標題檢索沒有命中能核對的那篇。

## 不寫的理由
- 關鍵說法「酒精會經皮膚吸收讓孩子中毒」沒有一份能逐字核對的原文。
- 剩下能核到的只是「別擦身降溫」，而 NICE、NHS 和國家衛健委 2016 年科普互相沖突，Cochrane 又說溫水擦身確實能降一點溫，只是讓孩子發抖。按「有爭議」寫進條目要兩側都擺出來，篇幅和價值都不夠單開一條。
- 第 13 節第 16 條已經寫了「孩子發燒抽搐時別拿冷水擦身降溫」，熱性驚厥那個最要緊的場景已有動作。
- 以後若取到 AAP 2011 全文或可靠的病例報告，可以按「孩子發燒別用酒精擦身」並進第 34 節第 2 條的備註，不單開條目。
