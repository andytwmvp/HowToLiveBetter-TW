# 追加：第 20 節第 16、17 條，嬰兒每天補維生素 D、洗澡防燙

2026-10-14。兩條都追加在第 20 節末尾，沒有插在中間。本節加完 17 條，不到 20 條，不補節首導覽。

## 查重
- 維生素 D：全書只有第 6 節第 3 條（不缺維生素 D 的人）講成人別為長壽去補，它的備註裡寫著「嬰幼兒還是按醫生說的補」，但沒有劑量、時間和理由。第 6 節第 30 條講骨折後補鈣補維 D。嬰兒怎麼補沒有條目，不算重複。新條目備註指回第 6 節第 3 條，反向指路請主任務補。
- 洗澡防燙：第 13 節第 14 條講燙傷後沖涼水（急救），第 2 節泡澡條講成人，第 20 節第 5 條講衝奶粉水溫。沒有講怎麼防嬰兒被洗澡水燙的條目，不算重複。

## 四問
- 維生素 D：① 每個新生兒家庭都會碰上；② 國內常見的誤區是 6 個月內就給孩子補鈣，或者覺得吃配方奶、曬太陽就不用補；③ 動作明確（每天補、6 個月內不補鈣、不自己加量）；④ 有衛健委原文和國際共識。
- 洗澡防燙：① 每個家庭都要給嬰兒洗澡；② 很少有人知道 60 ℃ 上下的熱水幾秒鐘就能燙成三度；③ 動作明確，本書沒有；④ 有 WHO 報告、美國 CPSC、AAP、NHS 原文。

## 第 16 條：維生素 D

### 1. 國家衛生健康委辦公廳 (2020) 嬰幼兒餵養健康教育核心信息
- URL：<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-08/01/content_5531915.htm>（2026-10-14 curl 直連取到）
- 原文：「嬰兒從出生開始，應當在醫生指導下每天補充維生素D 400-800國際單位，促進生長發育。正常足月嬰兒出生後6個月內一般不用補充鈣劑。」
- 國內寫的是 400 到 800，國際共識寫 400。標題寫 400，收益欄把國內原文照錄，說人話寫「量按醫生說的給」。

### 2. Munns CF 等 (2016) 佝僂病全球共識
- DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2015-2175>，JCEM 101(2):394-415，PMID 26745253，全文 PMC4880117（Europe PMC 全文接口返回 500，改從 pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov 帶代理取到）
- 推薦：「400 IU/d (10 μg) is adequate to prevent rickets and is recommended for all infants from birth to 12 months of age, independent of their mode of feeding. (1⊕⊕⊕)」（1 是強推薦，⊕⊕⊕ 是中等質量證據）
- 11 個組織：摘要「The consensus group, representing 11 international scientific organizations」。
- 土耳其數據：「no cases of radiographically confirmed rickets were seen after administration of 400 IU/d of vitamin D for 12 months, whereas the incidence was 3.8% in Turkish infants and young children who did not receive supplementation」。
- 全球無報告：「Worldwide, there have been no reports of radiographically confirmed rickets in infants or children receiving 400 IU on a regular, daily basis.」

### 3. Beser E, Cakmakci T (1994) East Afr Med J 71(6):358-362，PMID 7835254
- 摘要原文：「Rickets was not seen where 400 IU of vitamin D was administered, while incidence for the twelve-month period was calculated as 3.8% in the other group.」研究在土耳其東北部 21 個村子，3 到 36 個月的孩子。
- 不是隨機分組（一組給建議、一組給 400 IU），所以證據等級定 B。

### 4. Specker BL 等 (1992) J Pediatr 120(5):733-739，DOI 10.1016/s0022-3476(05)80236-7
- 摘要原文：「256 term infants from two northern and two southern cities in China were studied in a randomized trial of vitamin D supplementation (100, 200, or 400 IU/day) during the first 6 months of life.」；「Cord serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D concentrations were lower in the north than in the south (5 vs 14 ng/ml (12.5 vs 35.0 nmol/L)」；「None of the infants had rickets at 6 months of age.」；「it may be prudent to supplement the diet with vitamin D at a dose of 400 IU/day.」
- 12.5/35.0 ≈ 0.36，條目寫「約三分之一」。這項研究裡補 100 IU 的也沒得佝僂病，條目照實寫了「三組都沒有」。

### 5. Tan ML, Abrams SA, Osborn DA (2020) Cochrane CD013046.pub2，PMID 33305822
- 背景：「Vitamin D levels are low in breast milk and exclusively breastfed infants are at risk of vitamin D insufficiency or deficiency.」
- 結果：「vitamin D at 400 IU/day ... may reduce the incidence of vitamin D insufficiency (25-OH vitamin D < 50 nmol/L) (RR 0.57, 95% CI 0.41 to 0.80; participants = 274; studies = 4; low-certainty). However, there was insufficient evidence to determine if vitamin D given to the infant reduces the risk of vitamin D deficiency ... or the incidence of biochemical or radiological rickets (all very-low certainty).」
- RR 0.57 寫成「少約四成」。

### 6. AAP, Where We Stand: Vitamin D & Iron Supplements for Babies（Last Updated 5/24/2022）
- URL：<https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/feeding-nutrition/Pages/Vitamin-Iron-Supplements.aspx>（帶代理取到）
- 原文：「children should wear sunscreen, hats, and protective clothing when outdoors for extended periods of time ... For this reason, the AAP recommends that all infants receive a vitamin D supplement (unless they are consuming more than 27 ounces per day of commercial formula that has the vitamin D supplement added.」；「Vitamin D supplements of 400 IU (10 mcg) per day are recommended for babies up until age one year」
- 27 盎司 ≈ 798 毫升，條目寫「約 800 毫升」。和國內「所有嬰兒都補」不一致，寫在備註裡。

### 7. Gallo S 等 (2013) JAMA 309(17):1785-1792，DOI 10.1001/jama.2013.3404
- 摘要原文：「The 1600-IU/d dosage was discontinued prematurely because of elevated plasma 25(OH)D concentrations.」；「Growth and bone mineral content did not differ by dosage.」研究在加拿大蒙特利爾。

### 證據等級與收益量級
- B：共識是強推薦，但防佝僂病的數字來自沒有隨機分組的比較；Cochrane 合併的隨機試驗只看到維生素 D 水平的改善，看不出佝僂病少沒少。
- 收益中，憑判斷：3.8% 對 0 按機械規則會落「大」，但那是土耳其高危農村、非隨機；國內嬰兒佝僂病發生率沒查到。

## 第 17 條：洗澡防燙

### 1. US CPSC, Avoiding Tap Water Scalds (Document 5098)
- URL：<https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/5098.pdf>（帶代理下載，pdftotext 可抽）
- 原文：「Most adults will suffer third-degree burns if exposed to 150 degree water for two seconds. Burns will also occur with a six-second exposure to 140 degree water or with a thirty second exposure to 130 degree water. Even if the temperature is 120 degrees, a five minute exposure could result in third-degree burns.」
- 6 秒、30 秒兩檔原文只說 burns，沒說幾度，條目寫「也會燙傷」，只有 2 秒和 5 分鐘兩檔寫了三度。
- 換算：150 ℉ = 65.6 ℃，140 ℉ = 60 ℃，130 ℉ = 54.4 ℃，120 ℉ = 48.9 ℃。

### 2. AAP, Bathing Your Baby（Last Updated 7/6/2025）
- URL：<https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/bathing-skin-care/Pages/Bathing-Your-Newborn.aspx>
- 原文：「Fill the basin with 2 inches of water that feels warm—not hot—to the inside of your wrist or elbow.」；「Never bathe your baby in running water」；「The AAP recommends that the hottest temperature at the faucet should be no more than 120 degrees Fahrenheit to help avoid burns.」；「hot water scalds are the top cause of burns among babies and young children」；「Most child drownings inside the home occur in bathtubs, and more than half of bathtub deaths involve children under 1 year of age.」

### 3. NHS, Washing and bathing your baby（Page last reviewed 23 July 2024）
- URL：<https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/washing-and-bathing-your-baby/>
- 原文：「The water should be warm, not hot. Run the cold water first and then add hot water. Check it with your wrist or elbow and mix it well so there are no hot patches.」

### 4. WHO, UNICEF (2008) World report on child injury prevention, 第 4 章 Burns
- 題錄頁：<https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/43851>。頁面是前端渲染，正文用 DSpace 接口取：`/server/api/pid/find?id=10665/43851` 拿 uuid，`/server/api/core/items/<uuid>/bundles?embed=bitstreams` 列出英、中文 PDF 和對應的 .txt 文本層，直接下 .txt。
- 中文版原文：「嬰幼兒皮膚比成年人薄，較低的溫度就可能使之燒傷，而且燒傷的程度會更深（77）」
- 中文版原文：「在美國，通過教育計劃同控制熱水水龍頭溫度由60℃降至49℃的法規相結合的措施，華盛頓州因家用熱水導致的燙傷數量降低（111，112）。」
- 澳大利亞（中文版專題 4.4）：「全國範圍內淋浴間水龍頭的熱水溫度最高不超過50℃」；「從1989年到1996年，0-4歲年幼兒童因燙傷導致的住院比率降低了13%」；「最嚴重的燙傷（包括住院在10天及以上的）發生率下降最為明顯——在該運動第二階段之後的兩年內降低了30%」。英文版對應「hot water taps in bathrooms」，條目寫「浴室熱水龍頭」。
- 1 歲以下（英文版）：「Infants under the age of one year are still at significant risk for burns, even in developed countries. The burns they suffer are most commonly the result of scalds from cups containing hot drinks or contact burns from radiators or hot-water pipes」。這一段在講高收入國家，條目照寫「在高收入國家」。
- **沒有采用**：報告寫「加拿大的一項研究……發現該措施可降低56%的燙傷（58）」。核了第 58 號文獻，是 Han RK 等 (2007) Injury Prevention 13:248-253（DOI 10.1136/ip.2006.014480），摘要寫的是「a projected 56% reduction」，是對一項「proposed」措施的模型預測，不是實測，所以不寫。

### 5. Erdmann TC 等 (1991) Pediatrics 88(3):572-577，PMID 1881739
- 摘要原文：「Five years after a 1983 Washington State law required new water heaters to be preset at 49 degrees C (120 degrees F)」；「Mean temperature in 1988 was 50 degrees C compared with 61 degrees C in 1977.」；「Eighteen burn victims were identified from July 1979 through May 1988, for an average admission rate of 2.4 per year, compared with 5.5 per year in the 1970s. Compared with the 1970s, total body surface area burned, mortality, grafting, scarring, and length of hospital stay were all reduced」
- 2.4/5.5 = 0.44，降了 56%，說人話寫「少了一半多」。前後對比，不是分組試驗。

### 6. 國內兒童燒傷住院統計
- Xu JH 等 (2014) Burns 40(8):1780-1788：「A total of 61,068 cases were included」；「Children under 3 years old were at the highest risk of injury. Scalds were the commonest burns (87.59%).」
- Zhu L 等 (2013) Burns 39(5):1004-1011：「Among children hospitalized burns, the percentage of children younger than three years was 69.9%」；「Scald burns accounted for 89.79%」
- Han D 等 (2022) Front Public Health 10:751615：「scalds (90.63%)」；「The median age was 2 years」
- 三項都沒有把洗澡水單獨拆出來，條目寫「被熱的液體燙傷的佔 87.6% 到 90.6%」，不寫成洗澡水的比例。

### 7. WHO (2007) Legionella and the prevention of legionellosis
- 題錄頁：<https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/43233>，正文同樣走 DSpace 接口取 .txt。
- 原文：「In domestic and public hot-water systems, control measures for reducing the proliferation of Legionella must not increase the risk of scalding, particularly for children, the elderly or people with disabilities.」
- 原文：「The temperature of hot water reached within one minute at the tap should not be less than 50 ºC, except where thermostatic mixer valves are installed.」
- 原文（示例水安全計劃）：「Minimum flow temperature of 60°C to be maintained in water leaving the heating unit, and of 50°C at the tap」
- 原文：「This can be achieved by lowering water temperatures throughout distribution systems or by installing thermostatic mixing valves close to the point of water use.」
- 這條是標題裡「能設出水溫度的熱水器」那個限定的依據：儲水式熱水器調低溫度和防軍團菌衝突，條目讓儲水式的靠先冷後熱、試溫，或裝恆溫混水閥。

### 證據等級與收益量級
- B：華盛頓州和澳大利亞都是立法前後對比，CPSC 是燙傷閾值，沒有研究算過「試水溫」這個動作本身能少多少燙傷。
- 收益中，憑判斷：華盛頓州住院降 56% 按機械規則會落「大」，但那是改熱水器溫度的全州政策，不是家長個人動作；國內洗澡水燙傷沒有單獨數字。

## 沒收的
- 國內官方關於洗澡水溫、熱水器溫度的科普：國家衛健委站內搜索被限頻，沒取到，條目沒寫國內官方提示。
- 水溫計的價格：沒有來源，成本欄不寫價格。
