# 追加：第 6 節第 27 條補射精後疾病綜合徵（POIS）

2026-10-10。第 27 條原來只講「多數人手淫不傷身」。每次射精後都乏力、像感冒、幾天才好的讀者，看完會覺得自己被打發了。所以並進這一段，給他們一個就醫方向。沒有單開一條，因為新動作只有「每次都發作就去泌尿科或男科」，配不出單獨一條的成本和收益。證據等級、收益量級、成本標籤都不變。

題錄和卷期頁碼都按 Europe PMC 的 core 記錄核對。數字都出自摘要原文。

## 1. Waldinger 等 (2011) J Sex Med 8(4):1164-1170，PMID 21241453

- 「Of the 45 included men, 33 subjects consented with skin-prick testing. Of them, 29 (88%) men had a positive skin-prick test with their own (autologous) semen」
- 「In 87% of men, POIS symptoms started within 30 minutes after ejaculation.」
- 「a flu-like syndrome in 78% of subjects, exhaustion and concentration difficulties in 80% and 87% of subjects」。條目只用了 exhaustion 80%（寫成「疲憊沒力氣」）和 flu-like 78%。

## 2. Chea 等 (2023) J Sex Med 20(12):1407-1413，PMID 37872743

- 「Thirty-seven men were included in the study」「The mean duration of symptoms was 4.7 ± 3.4 days.」單中心、回顧性，地點是神經泌尿科（neurourology department）。條目寫「法國一家醫院」，按作者單位定的國別。

## 3. Xi 等 (2023) Sexual Medicine 11(6):qfad068，PMID 38239929

- 「The clinical characteristics of 24 Chinese patients with POIS were analyzed.」
- 「The patients and healthy donors all had positive intracutaneous test results with their own semen, negative skin prick test results, and no IgE specific to autologous semen.」條目寫「查不到針對精液的過敏抗體」。
- 「The SAS and SDS scores of the high and low VAS groups were significantly higher than those of the control group.」條目寫「焦慮抑鬱評分偏高」。

## 4. Ponce 等 (2025) J Sex Med 22(7):1236-1243，PMID 40434029

- 「Of 58 studies found, 14 matched our inclusion criteria. Our systematic review consists of 1 cross-sectional study, 1 cohort study, and 12 case reports.」

## 5. Duran 等 (2025) Int J Impot Res 37(6):454-457，PMID 37604984

- 「In total 211 sexual medicine experts filled the survey.」「Only 18% of participants reported symptom improvement in more than 30% of the patients.」

## 沒寫進條目的

- 患病率：沒有找到普通人群的統計。Li、Yin 2026 年的中國網絡調查（Int J Impot Res，PMID 42298129）裡，2443 人中有 770 人自述有類似症狀。但這項調查在社交媒體上招人，作者自己也寫了「Recruitment via social media may have introduced selection bias」，不能當患病率用。備註只寫「文獻都說這病罕見，但沒有人在普通人群裡統計過」。
- 心悸、胸口疼：上面幾篇摘要列的症狀組裡都沒有，沒寫。
- 具體療法（抗過敏藥、布洛芬、精液脫敏等）都只有個案，沒寫。
