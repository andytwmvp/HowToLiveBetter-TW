# 追加：第 27 節第 19、20 條，孕晚期側著入睡；產後避孕與下一胎間隔

2026-10-14。主任務給的兩個候選，都追加在第 27 節末尾，沒有插在中間。加完本節從 18 條到 20 條，按規則補了節首導覽，第 19 條掛「孕前和孕期自己能做的」，第 20 條掛「生完以後」。

## 查重與四問
- 全書 grep「仰睡、側睡、側臥、仰臥、死產、死胎」：沒有講孕婦睡姿的條目。第 20 節第 1 條講的是嬰兒仰睡，對象不同。
- 全書 grep「避孕、生育間隔、哺乳期」：第 13 節第 42 條講的是被性侵後的緊急避孕，不是同一件事。本節第 16 條（產後 42 天覆查）備註末尾只有一句「避孕方案也在這次談：產後不來月經，不等於不會懷孕」，沒有動作細節和數字。
- 第 20 條為什麼新開而不併進第 16 條：第 16 條的動作是「去做 42 天覆查」，第 20 條的動作是「同房就避孕、下一胎隔開」，動作不同。而且「餵奶就不會懷」流傳很廣，值得在標題裡出現；並進第 16 條備註只能留在最不顯眼的位置。兩條互相指路：第 20 條備註指向第 16 條（產後 42 天那次複查）。第 16 條備註本來就提了避孕，沒有再改它。
- 四問：兩條都是孕產婦普遍會碰上的；讀者普遍不知道（仰睡和死胎的關聯是 2010 年代才有的研究，「餵奶不會懷孕」是常見誤解）；動作是新的；有原始來源。

## 第 19 條：孕 28 周以後側著入睡

### 1. Cronin RS, et al. (2019). EClinicalMedicine 10:49-57. doi:10.1016/j.eclinm.2019.03.014（PMC6543252）
- Europe PMC 摘要與全文 XML，2026-10-14 取到。
- 「Six case-control studies were identified, with data obtained from five (cases, n = 851; controls, n = 2257).」
- 「Supine going-to-sleep position was associated with increased odds of late stillbirth (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 2.63, 95% CI 1.72-4.04, p < 0.0001) compared with left side. Right side had similar odds to left (aOR 1.04, 95% CI 0.83-1.31, p = 0.75).」
- 「There were no significant interactions between supine going-to-sleep position and assessed indicators of fetal vulnerability, including small-for-gestational-age infants (p = 0.32), maternal obesity (p = 0.08), and smoking (p = 0.86). The population attributable risk for supine going-to-sleep position was 5.8% (3.2-9.2).」
- 「This finding could reduce late stillbirth by 5.8% if every pregnant woman ≥ 28 weeks' gestation settled to sleep on her side.」
- 五項研究的地點（全文表 1）：Auckland, New Zealand；Sydney, Australia；New Zealand；United Kingdom；STARS 為 International，「Online survey」。條目寫成「四項在新西蘭、澳大利亞、英國，一項是網上的國際調查」。
- 作者自述侷限：「the exposure data and data about a number of prespecified confounders were collected retrospectively in the included case–control studies and subject to potential recall bias」；「there were no randomised trials or eligible prospective cohort studies identified for this IPD」。
- 基線：「Differences in the rates of late stillbirth between high-income countries [1] ranging from 1.3–8.8/1000 births」。

### 2. Silver RM, et al. (2019). Obstet Gynecol 134(4):667-676. doi:10.1097/AOG.0000000000003458（反方）
- Europe PMC 摘要。卷期頁碼經 Crossref 核對。
- 「A total of 8,706 (of 10,038) women had data from at least one sleep questionnaire and for pregnancy outcomes」，研究對象為「nulliparous women with singleton gestations」（條目寫「頭胎孕婦」）。
- 「There was no association between reported non-left lateral or supine sleep during the last week of the first visit (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 1.00 [95% CI 0.89-1.14]) or third visit (aOR 0.99 [95% CI 0.89-1.11] and the composite or any individual outcome, except for an apparent protective effect for stillbirth at the third visit (aOR 0.27 (95% CI 0.09-0.75).」
- 「Going to sleep in the supine or right lateral position, as self-reported before the development of pregnancy outcome and objectively assessed through 30 weeks of gestation, was not associated with an increased risk of stillbirth, a small-for-gestational-age newborn, or gestational hypertensive disorders.」
- 條目只寫「它只看到孕 30 周，後面幾周的睡姿沒有記錄」，這是標題和結論裡寫明的範圍。McCowan 等人 2020 年給這篇寫的批評信沒取到全文，批評的具體內容沒寫。

### 3. NHS. Stillbirth. <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stillbirth/>（Page last reviewed: 24 April 2024）
- 「not going to sleep on your back after 28 weeks – don't worry if you wake up on your back, just turn onto your side before you go back to sleep」
- 子頁 <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stillbirth/prevention/> 也有「going to sleep on your side, not on your back」，沒另引。
- RCOG 指南原文沒去取，NHS 已能逐字核對同一建議。

### 等級與量級
- 證據 B、爭議：全部數字來自病例對照研究、靠事後回憶，唯一一項提前記錄睡姿的大型研究只看到孕 30 周、結果為無關聯。
- 收益「大」：死亡率口徑按相對變化機械套，aOR 2.63 遠超 20% 的門檻。絕對風險很小（晚期死胎每 1000 次分娩 1.3 到 8.8 例，人群歸因 5.8%），備註已寫明。沒有改成「中」，是因為全書量級只按相對降幅套，證據可信度另由證據等級管。
- 成本：不花錢、改姿勢要點毅力，毅力=些。

## 第 20 條：產後避孕與下一胎間隔

### 4. NHS. Sex and contraception after birth. <https://www.nhs.uk/baby/support-and-services/sex-and-contraception-after-birth/>（Media last reviewed: 2 February 2026）
- 「You can get pregnant 3 weeks after the birth of a baby, including if you're breastfeeding and your periods haven't started again. You need to use contraception every time you have sex after giving birth unless you want to get pregnant again.」
- 「It's usually recommended that you wait at least 12 months after giving birth before becoming pregnant again.」
- 「You can start these types of contraception straight after the birth, including if you're breastfeeding: contraceptive implant contraceptive injection progestogen-only pill condoms female (internal) condoms IUD (coil) or IUS (hormonal coil)」；「If you're breastfeeding, you'll usually be advised to wait until at least 6 weeks after the birth before starting the combined pill, ring or patch.」
- 「You're unlikely to have any periods if you breastfeed exclusively (give your baby breast milk only) and your baby is under 6 months old. ... This is known as the lactational amenorrhoea method, or LAM.」
- 「It's important to start using another form of contraception if: your baby is more than 6 months old you give them anything else apart from breast milk, such as a dummy, formula or solid foods your periods start again (even light spotting counts) you stop night feeding you start to breastfeed less often there are longer intervals between feeds」

### 5. NHS. Natural family planning. <https://www.nhs.uk/contraception/methods-of-contraception/natural-family-planning/>
- 「If you are breastfeeding and follow lactational amenorrhea method correctly all the time, it is more than 99% effective at preventing pregnancy. If you do not follow the instructions exactly, it's 98% effective and 2 in 100 women will get pregnant again within 6 months of giving birth.」

### 6. Conde-Agudelo A, et al. (2006). JAMA 295(15):1809. doi:10.1001/jama.295.15.1809
- Europe PMC 摘要。
- 「Included studies were cohort, cross-sectional, and case-control studies with results adjusted for at least maternal age and socioeconomic status ... Of 130 articles identified in the search, 67 (52%) were included.」
- 「Compared with interpregnancy intervals of 18 to 23 months, interpregnancy intervals shorter than 6 months were associated with increased risks of preterm birth, low birth weight, and small for gestational age (pooled adjusted odds ratios [95% confidence intervals]: 1.40 [1.24-1.58], 1.61 [1.39-1.86], and 1.26 [1.18-1.33], respectively). Intervals of 6 to 17 months and longer than 59 months were also associated with a significantly greater risk for the 3 adverse perinatal outcomes.」
- 結束頁碼 Crossref 只給了起始頁 1809，來源欄只寫起始頁。

### 7. Schummers L, et al. (2018). JAMA Intern Med 178(12):1661. doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2018.4696
- 主任務提示裡把它列為反方，讀摘要後發現它是支持方：結論是「short interpregnancy intervals are associated with increased risks for adverse pregnancy outcomes for women of all ages」。條目把它放在收益欄，反方換成 Ball 2014（見下）。
- 「Among 148 544 pregnancies, maternal mortality or severe morbidity risks were increased at 6-month compared with 18-month interpregnancy intervals for women aged 35 years or older (0.62% at 6 months vs 0.26% at 18 months; aRR, 2.39; 95% CI, 2.03-2.80), but not for women aged 20 to 34 years (0.23% at 6 months vs 0.25% at 18 months; aRR, 0.92; 95% CI, 0.83-1.02).」
- 「Increased adverse fetal and infant outcome risks were more pronounced for women aged 20 to 34 years (2.0% at 6 months vs 1.4% at 18 months; aRR, 1.42; 95% CI, 1.36-1.47)」；這個合併結局的定義是「stillbirth, infant death, <third birthweight percentile for gestational age and sex, delivery <28 weeks」，條目寫成「死胎、夭折、極小或極早產合計」。
- 研究地點 British Columbia, Canada。

### 8. World Health Organization (2007). Report of a WHO Technical Consultation on Birth Spacing, Geneva, 13–15 June 2005（WHO/RHR/07.1）
- 頁面 <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-07.1>，PDF <https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/58173bae-0761-4d56-aebf-1e959f88da1d/content>，pdftotext 取到英文全文。
- 「After a live birth, the recommended interval before attempting the next pregnancy is at least 24 months in order to reduce the risk of adverse maternal, perinatal and infant outcomes.」
- 「After a miscarriage or induced abortion, the recommended minimum interval to next pregnancy is at least six months in order to reduce risks of adverse maternal and perinatal outcomes.」
- 「the participants fell into two groups: those who considered that the evidence indicated that the most suitable recommended interval was 18 months, and those who considered that the evidence supported a recommended interval of 27 months. Participants were, however, unanimous in agreeing that birth-to-pregnancy intervals shorter than 18 months should be avoided.」；「At the meeting, a compromise was reached between the two groups」。
- 標題裡的「至少隔 18 個月」取的是與會專家一致同意的這條下限，不是 24 個月的妥協值。

### 9. Ball SJ, et al. (2014). BMJ 349:g4333. doi:10.1136/bmj.g4333（反方）
- Europe PMC 摘要。地點 Perth, Western Australia，「40 441 mothers who each delivered three liveborn singleton neonates」，「matching two intervals per mother so each mother acts as her own control」。
- 「The traditional unmatched design estimated an adjusted odds ratio for an interpregnancy interval of 0-5 months (relative to the reference category of 18-23 months) of 1.41 (95% confidence interval 1.31 to 1.51) for preterm birth ... In comparison, the matched design showed a much weaker effect of short interpregnancy interval on preterm birth (odds ratio 1.07, 0.86 to 1.34) and low birth weight (1.03, 0.79 to 1.34)」
- 「This study questions the causal effect of short interpregnancy intervals on adverse birth outcomes and points to the possibility of unmeasured or inadequately specified maternal factors in previous studies.」

### 等級與量級
- 證據 B、爭議：全是跟蹤記錄的研究，沒有隨機分組；用同一位母親自己比的研究看不出短間隔的害處；各機構建議的間隔從 12 個月到 24 個月不等。「餵奶不等於避孕」這部分沒有爭議，來自 NHS。
- 收益「中」憑判斷：按閾值機械套，早產 OR 1.40 已過 20%，應是「大」。沒這麼定，因為 Ball 2014 用母親自身對照後，早產和低出生體重都縮到看不出差別；Schummers 2018 裡母親死亡或重病的升高只出在 35 歲以上。效應有多少是間隔本身造成的說不準，所以降一檔。
- 成本：避孕套幾十元，錢=少；毅力=些（每次都要用）。人群=懷孕（規範裡懷孕含產後一年）。
- 沒找到國內官方的產後避孕或生育間隔文件（gov.cn 政策庫按標題搜「產後避孕」「避孕節育」「哺乳期避孕」無結果），所以全用 NHS 和 WHO。
