## 第 21 條

### 四問
1. 會碰上：本節讀者裡脊髓損傷是常見致殘原因，幾乎都要面對排尿管理。
2. 不知道：家屬常以為插上尿管就一勞永逸，不知道要終身複查腎。
3. 動作是新的：本節第 1 條只講自主神經反射異常的現場處理。
4. 有原始來源：兩項期刊研究加歐洲泌尿外科學會指南原文。

### 1. Savic 2017，Spinal Cord 55(10):891-897，doi:10.1038/sc.2017.64，PMID 28631749
- 摘要取自 Europe PMC REST。
- 「The sample consisted of 5483 patients with tSCI admitted to Stoke Mandeville and Southport spinal centres who were injured between 1943 and 2010, survived first year post injury, had residual neurological deficit on discharge and were British residents. Mortality information, including causes of death, was collected up to 31 December 2014.」
- 「In total, 2322 persons (42.3% of the sample) died, with 2170 (93.5%) having a reliable cause of death established. The most frequent causes of death were respiratory (29.3% of all certified causes), circulatory, including cardiovascular and cerebrovascular diseases (26.7%), neoplasms (13.9%), urogenital (11.5%) ...」
- 「Compared to the general population, age-standardised cause-specific mortality rates were higher for all causes, especially skin, urogenital and respiratory; rates showed improvement over time for suicides, circulatory and urogenital causes」

### 2. Weld & Dmochowski 2000，J Urol 163(3):768-772，doi:10.1016/s0022-5347(05)67800-7，PMID 10687973
- 「We retrospectively reviewed the medical records, upper tract imaging and video urodynamics of 316 posttraumatic spinal cord injured patients. Mean followup plus or minus standard deviation since injury was 18.3+/-12.4 years.」
- 「Of the 398 complications recorded 236 developed in 61 (53.5%) patients on chronic urethral catheterization, 57 in 25 (27.2%) on clean intermittent catheterization, 57 in 24 (32.4%) who voided spontaneously and 48 in 16 (44.4%) on suprapubic catheterization.」
- 「Infection, stone disease, urethral complications and radiographic abnormalities were recorded.」
- 回顧研究，沒有隨機分組，正文已寫明。

### 3. EAU Guidelines on Neuro-Urology（2026 版）
- URL：<https://uroweb.org/guidelines/neuro-urology/chapter/the-guideline>，靜態抓取只有殼頁，帶代理用無頭 Chrome dump DOM 取到正文。頁面附的全文 PDF 文件名為 Neuro-Urology-2026.pdf，據此定為 2026 版。
- 3.4.2.e.1：「Indwelling transurethral catheterisation and, to a lesser extent, suprapubic cystostomy are associated with a range of complications as well as an enhanced risk for UTI [...]. Therefore, both procedures should be avoided, when possible. Silicone catheters are preferred」
- 同節（反方，寫進備註「爭議」）：「Intermittent and indwelling catheterisation are standard treatments for patients who are unable to empty their bladder.」（LE 3）；推薦「Use a shared decision-making approach when discussing intermittent or indwelling catheterisation」（Strong）；「in the SCI population, IC is associated with worse reported QoL compared to indwelling catheters, particularly if recurrent (> 4 per year) UTIs complicate management」；「Adequate hand function is an independent risk factor for cessation of intermittent catheterisation (IC)」。
- 同節頻次：「The average frequency of catheterisations per day is four to six times」，成本欄「一天四到六次」出自這裡。
- 3.3 節（診斷評估）開頭：「Long-term follow-up (life-long) is mandatory to assess risk of UUT damage and renal failure」
- 3.3.8：「Patients with SCI or SB have a higher risk of developing renal failure compared with patients with slowly progressive non-traumatic neurological disorders」
- 3.7：「the interval between initial investigations and control diagnostics [...] in many cases, should not exceed one to two years. [...] The UUT should be checked by means of ultrasonography at regular intervals in high-risk patients approximately once every six months [...]. In these patients, physical examination and urine laboratory should be carried out every year」
- 3.5（備註裡的感染信號）：「The most common signs and symptoms suspicious of a UTI [...] are fever, new onset or increase in incontinence, including leaking around an indwelling catheter, increased spasticity, malaise, lethargy or sense of unease, cloudy urine with increased urine odour [...]」

### 定級
- 證據等級 B：兩項研究都是觀察性的，指南對導尿方式的證據等級是 LE 3，複查間隔是 LE 4。指南本身也說間歇導尿和留置尿管都是標準做法，所以標「爭議」。
- 收益量級「中」，憑判斷：Weld 的併發症比例差約一半，按閾值夠「大」；但它是回顧對比，沒扣掉選擇偏差，終點也只是併發症不是死亡。Savic 只說明泌尿生殖死因佔一成多，沒比較不同做法。所以降一檔定中。
- 沒取到：國內《脊髓損傷神經源性膀胱診療指南》原文；美國脊髓醫學聯盟 2006 年膀胱管理指南（PMC1949036）全文 PDF 抓下來是跳轉殼頁，沒引。

### 4. 工傷保險輔助器具配置管理辦法（人社部、民政部、國家衛生計生委令第 27 號，2016-04-01 施行）
- URL：<https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2016/content_5074060.htm>
- 第二條：「工傷職工因日常生活或者就業需要，經勞動能力鑑定委員會確認，配置假肢、矯形器、假眼、假牙和輪椅等輔助器具的，適用本辦法。」
- 第十五條：「工傷職工配置輔助器具的費用包括安裝、維修、訓練等費用，按照規定由工傷保險基金支付。」
- 第二十四條：「有下列情形之一的，經辦機構不予支付配置費用：（一）未經勞動能力鑑定委員會確認，自行配置輔助器具的；（二）在非協議機構配置輔助器具的；（三）配置輔助器具超目錄或者超出限額部分的；（四）違反規定更換輔助器具的。」
- 2016 年之後有沒有修訂沒查到，條文按公報原文引。
